## Approved caption

What does morning daylight have to do with your body clock? Research describes light as one of the environmental cues linked with circadian timing. For people on a regular night-sleep schedule, bright morning light can shift timing earlier, while bright evening light can shift it later. Sleep is influenced by many factors, so this is context, not a one-size-fits-all rule.

Sources: CDC/NIOSH; Blume, Garbazza & Spitschan (2019); Wright et al. (2013).
