# Production Plan: Sleep, Stress, and the Brain

**Status:** Approved under the standing zero-touch policy as a non-diagnostic educational draft. The production choice is a 60-second, vertical, Hinglish science explainer for a general adult audience. It will not be submitted for publication without a separate official-account publishing path and required platform confirmation.

## Global creative specification

| Dimension | Production decision |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Explain the bidirectional relationship between sleep and stress without diagnosis or treatment claims. |
| Format | 60 seconds, 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 target. |
| Language | Hinglish off-screen narration with English on-screen accessibility captions added during assembly. |
| Visual style | Calm photorealistic editorial science explainer: navy, indigo, muted teal, and warm sunrise gold; soft cinematic lighting; modest detail density. |
| Recurring visual element | A generic, non-identifiable South Asian adult in plain navy sleepwear; not modeled on the user or any real patient. |
| Sound | Calm neutral Hinglish narrator, no on-screen dialogue, light ambient instrumental bed kept below narration. |
| Safety | No diagnosis, no medical treatment claim, no supplements, no imagery of self-harm, and a clinician boundary at the end. |

## Six-clip plan

| Clip | Time | Purpose | Scene and action | Camera and continuity | Narration cue |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10s | Establish | Generic adult lies awake briefly in a quiet blue bedroom; a gentle neural-light motif appears above the head, then calms. | Slow push-in, portrait medium-wide. Scene cut in. | `Aap jab sote ho, aapka brain off nahi hota. It is still working.` |
| 2 | 10–20s | Develop | Abstract but realistic neural pathways organize into warm memory-like lights, then resolve to a sleeping silhouette. | Slow orbit around floating pathways. Scene cut. | `Sleep ke dauran brain learning, memory aur next-day focus ko support karta hai.` |

## Detailed transition descriptions

### Clip 1
The generic adult in plain navy sleepwear is already lying on a neatly made bed in a quiet indigo bedroom at the beginning of the shot. The camera performs a slow, stable push-in while a subtle, translucent neural-light motif remains above the person’s head without forming medical imagery; the motif gradually softens as their breathing becomes calm. The bedside lamp, pale curtains, and uncluttered room remain present throughout. No text, labels, logos, or clinical equipment appears.

### Clip 2
A constellation of soft teal neural pathways is visible from the first frame against a dark navy background, with warm memory-like points of light already distributed across the network. The camera makes a slow, physically plausible arc while the pathways gently organize and connect, then dissolve into the silhouette of the same sleeping adult. The color palette stays muted teal and indigo, and the neural network remains visible until it gradually fades rather than popping out. No literal brain scan, clinical chart, text, or label appears.

### Clip 3
A bedside clock, a soft grey thought cloud, and a smooth animated breathing line are all present from the beginning in a calm split composition. The camera moves laterally at a slow rate while the thought cloud briefly becomes more active, the clock advances modestly, and the breathing line returns to an even rhythm. Every element remains in frame or transitions by an intentional soft dissolve; nothing alarming, chaotic, or diagnostic appears. The visual communicates a loop without depicting panic, illness, or self-harm.

### Clip 4
The shot begins with dim blue room light at a bedside level, with the generic adult and closed curtains already visible. The camera tilts upward as the person opens the curtains and warm sunrise light spreads across the same room, followed by a simple unlabelled circular range animation integrated as a visual accent. The room, person, and window remain physically consistent throughout, and the sunrise gradually brightens rather than appearing abruptly. No number or health claim is rendered inside the generated image; editorial captions will be added later.

### Clip 5
The face-down phone, warm bedside lamp, closed book, and glass of water are placed on the table from the first frame. The camera tracks slowly across the objects as a hand gently turns the lamp off, then closes the book, retaining the same object positions and warm-to-dim lighting transition. Nothing is removed suddenly and no branded device, medicine, supplement, or medical symbol appears. The shot ends on a quiet, uncluttered bedside scene.

### Clip 6
The generic adult is already sitting calmly at the edge of the bed as warm dawn light fills the room from the start. The camera slowly pulls back while the person takes one natural breath and looks toward the window, then the scene dissolves into a soft indigo-to-gold gradient that remains on screen. No claim of cure, diagnosis, or transformed health is implied. No text, watermark, logo, or label appears in the generated footage.
