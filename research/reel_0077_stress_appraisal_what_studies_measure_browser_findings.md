# R0077 browser research findings

## Scope
Topic: तनाव-मूल्यांकन: अध्ययन वास्तव में क्या मापते हैं. Unique angle: how stress appraisal studies operationalize perceived stress, exposure/events, physiological signals, and outcomes. The reel must distinguish subjective appraisal, stressors, physiology, and association; it must not diagnose, prescribe, or imply that one score reveals a person’s true stress or health.

## Opened sources

### [1] Harris et al. (2023), Annals of Behavioral Medicine / PMC
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10498818/
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/abm/kaad015
PMID: 37084792

The paper describes the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) as a widely used measure designed to assess perceptions of recent stress. It used repeated assessments, up to 13 PSS assessments, in two samples: 127 patients with heart failure followed over 39 months and 73 younger healthy adults followed over 12 months. Multilevel models estimated between-person and within-person variance. Between-person variance accounted for 42.3% of total PSS variance in the heart-failure sample and 51.1% in the healthy-adult sample; the paper’s conclusion is that the construct may substantially reflect a relatively stable characteristic of how a person perceives stressful life circumstances, while within-person variation also exists.

Measurement boundary: repeated PSS scores are self-reported perceptions; they are not a direct readout of the number of objective stressors or a physiological stress response. The samples and repeated-measure design bound the inference.

### [2] Lee (2012), Review of the Psychometric Evidence of the Perceived Stress Scale
URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1976131712000527
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anr.2012.08.004

The review examined psychometric evidence for the PSS and reports that the search ultimately identified 19 articles. It characterizes the PSS as an easy-to-use questionnaire with acceptable psychometric properties overall, while noting that the evidence was drawn mostly from college-student and worker populations and that future validation in diverse populations is needed.

Measurement boundary: psychometric acceptability does not mean the questionnaire measures every aspect of stress, works identically in every population, or substitutes for event exposure, clinical assessment, or physiological measurement.

### [3] Vaessen et al. (2021), PLOS ONE systematic review
URL: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0259557
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0259557

This descriptive systematic review evaluated studies that assessed self-reported stress and physiological measures in daily life, using repeated assessments over at least one day. The extracted abstract reports that 35% of all analyses showed a significant or marginally significant association in the expected direction. The most consistent results were found for perceived stress, high-arousal negative-affect scales, event-related self-reported stress measures, and frequency-domain heart-rate-variability measures.

Measurement boundary: an association across analyses is not identity or causality. Subjective stress and cardiovascular/HRV measures can converge in some contexts while remaining different signals with different timing and sources of variation.

### [4] Grimm, Francia & Agrigoroaei (2024), Assessment
URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10731911231223120
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/10731911231223120

The abstract states that the Stress Appraisal Measure (SAM) captures six types of cognitive appraisals in anticipation of an upcoming stressful situation. In a French-language validation, Study 1 (N=425) supported removing five items, combining threat and challenge subscales, and a new general five-factor structure; Study 2 (N=308) confirmed the new structure and construct, convergent, and discriminant validity. The authors also discuss prior instability of factor structures and low internal consistency for some subscales in other-language validation studies.

Measurement boundary: an appraisal scale operationalizes how a person evaluates an anticipated situation; it is not a direct measure of objective stress exposure or a universal physiological state. The validation is language- and sample-bound, and the paper itself shows that instrument structure can change across studies.

## Evidence synthesis for R0077

Stress appraisal studies can measure at least four different layers: exposure or events, a person’s perceived appraisal of recent or anticipated circumstances, physiological signals such as cardiovascular/HRV measures, and downstream outcomes. A PSS score is a self-report perception, not the event itself. A SAM score is a structured appraisal of an upcoming situation, not proof that the situation is objectively threatening. Physiology may show associations with self-reported stress in some analyses, but the signals are not interchangeable and do not by themselves diagnose a mental or medical condition. The reel should state the instrument, time window, population, and outcome before interpreting a result.
