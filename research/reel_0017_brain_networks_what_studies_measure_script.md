## Evidence distinctions

- **Structural connectivity:** white-matter tractography या anatomical relationships से physical organization का model।
- **Functional connectivity:** separated regions की measured time series के बीच statistical dependence या co-variation।
- **Effective/causal inference:** direction या influence की claim के लिए अतिरिक्त model और assumptions; ordinary correlation से अलग।
- **Task activation:** defined experimental contrast में signal change; complex behavior का single-cause proof नहीं।
- **EEG/MEG:** electrical/magnetic activity के time-resolved measures, जिनकी spatial resolution और source-localization limits होती हैं।
- **fMRI/BOLD:** hemodynamic/metabolic signal का indirect measure of neural functioning।
- **Graph metrics:** degree, strength, modularity, hubness, efficiency आदि selected graph का mathematical summary।
- **Atlas/parcellation:** nodes कैसे define किए गए, इससे quantitative network properties बदल सकती हैं।
- **Interpretation limit:** network statistic thought-reading, diagnosis, intelligence score या universal causal map नहीं है।

## Four-scene visual plan

### Scene 1 — Nodes and edges

A conceptual whole-brain silhouette is represented as a clean network of colored nodes and edges. A separate atlas grid sits beside it, emphasizing that the network is a chosen representation rather than a literal complete wiring map.

### Scene 2 — Structural versus functional

Two adjacent panels show white-matter tract bundles on one side and two synchronized but separate time-series traces on the other. A small distinction marker prevents the viewer from equating co-variation with a physical cable.

### Scene 3 — Signals and graph metrics

A task card, an abstract BOLD waveform, and an EEG trace feed into a connection matrix. A graph summary card shows generic icons for degree, modules, hubs, and efficiency without clinical labels or fabricated numbers.

### Scene 4 — Methodological guardrail

Atlas, preprocessing, threshold, and null-model cards surround a bounded box labelled “defined data + analysis choices”. A stop symbol blocks “thought reading”, “single cause”, and “diagnosis”.

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose neurological or psychiatric disorders from network language or imaging. Do not claim a brain region or network is the sole cause of intelligence, personality, behavior, or a symptom. Do not present functional connectivity as physical wiring or causation. Do not state that fMRI directly records neurons or private thoughts. No individualized scan interpretation, treatment, or prescription is included.
