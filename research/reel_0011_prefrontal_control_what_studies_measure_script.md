# Reel 0011 — प्रीफ्रंटल नियंत्रण: studies क्या measure करती हैं?

## Unique angle

प्रीफ्रंटल नियंत्रण को एक single “willpower button” मानने के बजाय दिखाना कि studies अलग-अलग tasks में inhibition, interference control, working-memory updating, switching और planning को operationalize करती हैं। Performance, BOLD activity और connectivity अलग evidence layers हैं।

## Hindi narration

### Hook — लगभग 0–7 सेकंड

“क्या prefrontal cortex आपके self-control का एक single button है? Research में control को कई अलग tasks से measure किया जाता है।”

### Body — लगभग 7–43 सेकंड

“कुछ studies Go/No-Go या stop-signal task में देखती हैं कि व्यक्ति response रोक पाता है या नहीं। Stroop task में conflicting information के बीच rule follow करने का समय और accuracy मापी जाती है। Working memory में information hold और update करनी होती है; task-switching में rule बदलना होता है।”

“फिर brain data जोड़ा जाता है। fMRI का BOLD signal conditions के बीच compare होता है। Multivariate analysis patterns classify करती है। Connectivity methods regions के statistical relationships estimate करती हैं।”

### Caveat और ending — लगभग 43–69 सेकंड

“लेकिन performance, BOLD activation और connectivity—तीनों एक ही चीज़ नहीं हैं। 2021 review control में unity और diversity बताती है: कुछ common हिस्सा है, पर task-specific demands भी हैं। Meta-analysis में कई executive tasks के दौरान fronto-cingulo-parietal convergence मिला, फिर भी domain-specific differences दिखे। इसलिए एक scan या एक score से किसी की पूरी discipline या self-control तय नहीं होता।”

“यह educational reel है; diagnosis या treatment advice नहीं। Visuals conceptual हैं, literal brain recording नहीं।”

## Evidence distinctions

- **Observed performance:** accuracy, reaction time, stop-signal reaction time, switching cost, working-memory performance, and error rate.
- **Neural measures:** BOLD contrasts, multivariate pattern classification, and statistical connectivity.
- **Synthesis:** coordinate-based meta-analysis estimates convergence across published studies.
- **Interpretation limit:** a task score is not the whole construct; activation is not a direct readout of thought, character, or discipline.

## Four-scene visual plan

### Scene 1 — Multiple control tasks

A vertical conceptual lab-board with four clean cards: stop signal, color-word conflict, memory update, and rule switch. Each card uses abstract symbols rather than real participant data. A central label reads “different tasks, different operations”.

### Scene 2 — Performance measures

Four panels show a reaction-time stopwatch, accuracy bars, a stop/cancel icon, and a changing rule card. Keep values qualitative and illustrative; do not show invented numerical results. On-screen label: “behavioural performance”.

### Scene 3 — Neural signal layers

A stylized brain-outline silhouette paired with a soft BOLD waveform, a pattern-grid classifier, and connected nodes labelled “statistical estimate”. Explicitly avoid depicting a thought, personality, or character trait as directly visible.

### Scene 4 — Unity and diversity guardrail

A network of several regions converges into a shared center while separate branches remain visible. A checklist asks: “Task क्या था?”, “क्या measure हुआ?”, and “क्या inference बचता है?” End with “एक score = पूरा self-control नहीं”.

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose ADHD, depression, PTSD, impulsivity, or any condition from task performance or brain signals. Do not claim that the prefrontal cortex is the sole controller of behavior, that a scan measures willpower or character, or that training guarantees improved control. Do not present a correlation or activation contrast as a causal explanation of a person’s behavior.
