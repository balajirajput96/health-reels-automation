## Evidence distinctions

- **Subjective report:** visibility rating, perceptual-awareness scale, confidence या verbal report।
- **Objective performance:** forced-choice discrimination, detection accuracy, d-prime और reaction time।
- **Masking/dissociation:** visible बनाम masked stimuli तथा awareness और priming contrasts।
- **Report-based neural measures:** EEG/ERP, fMRI, MEG या intracranial signals aligned to reported awareness।
- **No-report proxies:** eye movements, pupil, physiology, imaging या passive-viewing measures।
- **Computational/decoding models:** signal-detection, Bayesian models और classifiers।
- **Interpretation limit:** neural correlate association है; automatically cause, diagnosis या personal score नहीं।

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not claim EEG, fMRI, intracranial recordings, pupil measures, eye movements, or performance tasks directly read subjective experience or diagnose consciousness in an individual. Do not equate chance performance with absolute unconsciousness. Do not present research correlates as causes, clinical assessments, or mind-reading devices. No treatment, prescription, or personalized advice is included.
