## Evidence distinctions

- **Glucose use:** FDG-PET uptake, SUV या regional cerebral metabolic rate of glucose।
- **Blood flow:** Oxygen-15 water PET, arterial-spin labeling या related perfusion measures।
- **Oxygen metabolism:** Oxygen-15 PET या flow-and-oxygen models से estimate।
- **BOLD-fMRI:** Oxy/deoxyhemoglobin और hemodynamics से जुड़ा surrogate signal।
- **Metabolic flux:** MRS, stable-isotope tracing, microdialysis या nanosensors से selected intermediates और pathways।
- **Cellular metabolism:** Purified cells, culture, slices और animal assays में cell-specific pathways।
- **Multimodal correlation:** PET और fMRI signals का statistical association; same measurement या causality नहीं।
- **Individual inference:** Group-level or model-derived energy measures को viewer का personal score नहीं मानना।

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose or assess mental effort, intelligence, fatigue, treatment response, or future performance from PET, MRI, MRS, blood flow, oxygen, glucose, lactate, or other research signals. Do not claim BOLD is direct neuronal firing or that FDG uptake is a complete ATP readout. Do not present model-derived or group-level values as an individual’s personal brain-energy score. No treatment, prescription, or personalized advice is included.
