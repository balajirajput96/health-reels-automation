## Verified source record

### [1] Morin (2011), *Self-awareness part 1: Definition, measures, effects, functions, and antecedents*

- DOI: `10.1111/j.1751-9004.2011.00387.x`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-9004.2011.00387.x
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed conceptual review.
- Relevant boundary: self-awareness is multidimensional; measurement approaches include questionnaires, implicit tasks, and self-recognition. These measures target related but non-identical components such as self-focused attention, self-knowledge, self-recognition, or reflective processing.

### [2] Mograbi et al. (2024), *The cognitive neuroscience of self-awareness: Current advances and future directions*

- DOI: `10.1002/wcs.1670`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1002/wcs.1670
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed cognitive-neuroscience review.
- Relevant boundary: self-awareness is not a single construct. Research examines neurocognitive mechanisms through several domains and methods, and self-awareness should not be reduced to one region, questionnaire score, or imaging pattern.

### [3] Salvato et al. (2019), *Building the bodily self-awareness*

- DOI: `10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02727`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02727
- Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7268061/
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed review.
- Relevant boundary: bodily self-awareness concerns the experience of conscious states as bound to a unitary self. Studies use multisensory bodily illusions, body ownership, agency, perspective, and interoceptive or sensorimotor measures. These are operational proxies for specific bodily-self processes, not a complete self-awareness meter.

### [4] Huttunen et al. (2017), *Can self-awareness be taught? Monkeys pass the mirror test*

- DOI: `10.1073/pnas.1701676114`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1701676114
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed mirror-self-recognition methodology and interpretation paper.
- Relevant boundary: mirror self-recognition is often treated as evidence related to self-awareness, but the interpretation depends on task design, behavior, species, learning history, and what aspect of self-processing is intended. Passing or failing a mirror task should not be treated as a complete verdict on an organism’s entire self-awareness.

## Evidence map for the reel

| Measurement layer | Typical observable | What it can support | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-report / questionnaire | Self-focused attention, self-knowledge, reflective or private-experience ratings | Reported aspects of self-related thought under specified items | A bias-free, stable, or complete measure of self-awareness |
| Self-recognition | Mirror, photograph, video, or body-action recognition behavior | Recognition of a self-related visual or action cue in a task | Full self-awareness, consciousness, or a species-wide mental verdict |
| Metacognitive monitoring | Confidence, error awareness, calibration, discrepancy between confidence and accuracy | Monitoring of one’s own performance or knowledge | All forms of self-awareness or a direct readout of subjective selfhood |
| Bodily self / agency | Ownership, agency, perspective, multisensory illusion, interoceptive accuracy | Specific components of bodily self-processing | A single unified self mechanism or personal diagnosis |
| Neural measures | EEG/ERP, fMRI, connectivity, stimulation or autonomic physiology | Neural and physiological correlates of self-related processing in a paradigm | Direct mind-reading, personal self-awareness score, or causal proof from correlation |
| Social / implicit measures | Self-reference effect, implicit association, action attribution, observer judgments | Effects of self-related information or attribution processes | A complete model of identity, personality, or inner life |

## Source and safety boundary

The reel treats self-awareness as a multidimensional research construct. It distinguishes self-report, self-recognition, metacognitive monitoring, bodily self-processing, social/implicit measures, and neural correlates. No single questionnaire, mirror behavior, illusion, EEG/fMRI pattern, or interoceptive task is presented as a universal self-awareness test.

This is public education, not diagnosis, treatment, prescription, or personalized advice. No clinical or personality inference is made from a research task, and no social publishing is authorized by Drive storage.
