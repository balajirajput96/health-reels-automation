# Reel 0013 — आंतरिक संवाद: studies वास्तव में क्या मापती हैं?

## Unique angle

Inner speech को “दिमाग की एक लगातार चलती आवाज़” मानने के बजाय दिखाना कि research अलग-अलग windows इस्तेमाल करती है: generalized questionnaire, random experience sampling, behavioral interference, body signals, और brain correlates। हर window inner speech का अलग पहलू पकड़ती है।

### Hook — लगभग 0–6 सेकंड

### Body — लगभग 6–47 सेकंड

“एक questionnaire पूछ सकता है कि आम तौर पर आपका inner speech कैसा होता है—जैसे dialogue, self-evaluation या condensed phrases। लेकिन general estimate आपकी याद और अपनी image से प्रभावित हो सकता है।”

“Experience sampling में phone random समय पर पूछता है: इसी moment में आपके thoughts किस form में थे? एक study में general questionnaire और random reports हर feature पर एक जैसे नहीं थे। इसका मतलब यह नहीं कि कोई एक method हमेशा सही है; दोनों अलग time-windows पूछते हैं।”

“Researchers articulatory suppression भी इस्तेमाल करते हैं—जैसे words बोलकर verbal rehearsal को interfere करना—और देखते हैं कि memory या language task पर क्या असर पड़ता है।”

### Caveat और ending — लगभग 47–71 सेकंड

“EMG, EEG या fMRI शरीर और brain signals के correlates दिखा सकते हैं। लेकिन task में instructed inner speech, रोज़मर्रा के spontaneous inner speech जैसा हो, यह मान लेना सुरक्षित नहीं।”

“इसलिए पूछिए: method ने self-report, momentary experience, task effect या neural correlate में से क्या मापा? Inner speech का कोई universal meter या literal mind-reading अभी नहीं है। यह educational reel है; diagnosis या treatment advice नहीं।”

## Evidence distinctions

- **Phenomenon:** subjective language or verbal thinking without audible speech.
- **Generalized self-report:** questionnaire ratings about typical forms or qualities.
- **Momentary self-report:** random experience sampling of the current thought episode.
- **Behavioral proxy:** effects of articulatory suppression, verbal interference, or language tasks.
- **Physiological/neural proxy:** EMG, EEG, fMRI, neurostimulation, or decoding signals during specified tasks.
- **Interpretation limit:** each method samples a different window and may be affected by recall, language, compliance, task instructions, or context.

## Four-scene visual plan

### Scene 1 — Questionnaire window

A vertical conceptual panel shows a questionnaire card with four abstract inner-speech qualities represented by dialogue bubbles, a reflective mirror, abbreviated lines, and a small rating scale. The visual communicates self-report without showing a person’s private thoughts as readable text.

### Scene 2 — Experience sampling

A smartphone alert sends a momentary prompt into a timeline with several empty and filled observation points. A separate generalized estimate card sits above it, and the two paths remain visibly distinct to show that they sample different time windows.

### Scene 3 — Behavioral interference

A word-rehearsal loop is interrupted by a repeated spoken-token icon, then feeds into a memory/language task panel. The scene depicts an experimental manipulation and an observed task effect, not a diagnosis or proof of how every mind works.

### Scene 4 — Correlates and guardrail

Abstract EMG waveform, EEG trace, and fMRI-like network nodes appear beside a task prompt. A funnel labels the output “correlate / bounded inference,” followed by a stop sign for “literal mind-reading.”

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose anxiety, psychosis, dissociation, memory disorders, or other conditions from inner-speech reports, unusual experiences, or neural signals. Do not equate inner speech with hearing voices in a clinical sense. Do not claim that brain imaging or decoding can generally read private thoughts. Do not claim that one questionnaire measures total inner speech or that a behavioral effect proves a single mechanism.
