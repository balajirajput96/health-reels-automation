## Evidence distinctions

- **Directly observed:** controlled cue/context, participant ratings or behaviour, skin conductance, and scanner time-series data.
- **Task-derived:** context contrasts, conditioned-response differences, and statistical effect estimates.
- **Indirect/model-based:** BOLD activity and connectivity estimates; neither is a direct recording of thoughts or a complete emotion label.
- **Interpretation limit:** task design, sample, anatomical resolution, reliability, and model assumptions influence what a result means.

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose anxiety, PTSD, or any other condition from amygdala activity. Do not claim that the amygdala is the sole fear center, that BOLD directly measures thoughts or emotions, or that connectivity is direct neural wiring. Do not promise that any intervention changes a person’s amygdala or emotional life. Keep experimental findings, review conclusions, and model-based interpretations distinct.
