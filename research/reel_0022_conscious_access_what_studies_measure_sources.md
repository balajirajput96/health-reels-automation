# Reel 0022 — चेतन पहुँच: studies वास्तव में क्या मापते हैं?

## Verified source record

### [1] Jimenez, Prieto, Hinojosa & Montoro (2024), *Consciousness Under the Spotlight: The Problem of Measuring Subjective Experience*

- DOI: `10.1002/wcs.1697`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1002/wcs.1697
- Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11652689/
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed methodological review.
- Relevant boundary: consciousness research uses objective, performance-based measures and subjective, experience-based reports. Both have limitations. Subjective reports can be affected by response criteria and metacognitive demands; objective performance is an indirect measure and need not equal phenomenal experience. Masked-prime dissociation paradigms have sensitivity, relevance, immediacy, and statistical-interpretation challenges.

### [2] Duman et al. (2022), *The No-Report Paradigm: A Revolution in Consciousness Research?*

- DOI: `10.3389/fnhum.2022.861517`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2022.861517
- Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9130851/
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed critical review.
- Relevant boundary: no-report paradigms use eye movements, pupil, physiology, or neuroimaging as indirect indicators in an effort to reduce report-related confounds. Removing an overt report does not automatically remove attention, metacognitive reflection, or other alternative explanations. The review argues that no-report and report paradigms each have methodological trade-offs.

### [3] Dellert et al. (2021), *Dissociating the Neural Correlates of Consciousness and Task Relevance in Face Perception Using Simultaneous EEG-fMRI*

- DOI: `10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2799-20.2021`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2799-20.2021
- Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8445054/
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed simultaneous EEG-fMRI human experiment.
- Relevant boundary: the study compared awareness and task relevance using physically matched phases. Awareness-related effects and task-related effects were not identical; neural signals such as the N170, visual-awareness negativity, P3b, and fMRI activation patterns were interpreted as candidates or correlates under a specific paradigm, not as a universal consciousness meter.

### [4] Gaillard et al. (2009), *Converging Intracranial Markers of Conscious Access*

- DOI: `10.1371/journal.pbio.1000061`
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1000061
- Full text: https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.1000061
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed intracranial electrophysiology study.
- Relevant boundary: visually masked and unmasked words were compared in patients undergoing clinically indicated intracranial recordings. The study measured forced-choice performance and neural measures such as voltage, gamma power, phase synchrony, and Granger-causality estimates. Masking also changes stimulus processing, so differences are not a perfectly pure consciousness-only contrast; results are paradigm- and sample-specific.

## Evidence map for the reel

| Measurement layer | Typical observable | What it can support | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subjective report | Visibility rating, perceptual-awareness scale, confidence or verbal report | What the participant says they experienced under defined instructions | A bias-free or complete readout of phenomenal experience |
| Objective performance | Forced-choice discrimination, detection accuracy, d-prime, reaction time | Sensitivity to information and task-relevant access | That above-chance behavior is conscious experience or that chance means absolute absence of awareness |
| Masking / dissociation | Visible versus masked stimuli, awareness task versus priming task | A contrast between reportability, performance, and stimulus conditions | A pure consciousness-only contrast, because masking and task demands alter processing |
| Report-based neural correlates | EEG/ERP, fMRI, MEG, intracranial signals aligned to reported awareness | Neural activity associated with reported conscious versus less-conscious trials | A minimal sufficient cause or a general consciousness meter |
| No-report proxy | Eye movements, pupil, physiology, neuroimaging, passive-viewing behavior | Indirect inference about perceptual changes without an immediate verbal report | Proof of subjective experience, because alternative explanations and metacognitive processes remain |
| Computational / decoding model | Signal-detection measures, Bayesian models, classifiers, neural decoding | Quantitative description or prediction of patterns in data | Direct access to qualia or a theory proven by one successful model fit |

## Source and safety boundary

The reel uses “conscious access” as an operational research term. Studies typically compare report, performance, stimulus visibility, masking, and neural or physiological signals. They do not possess a universal device that directly measures another person’s subjective experience. Neural correlates are associations within a paradigm; they are not automatically causes, diagnoses, or personal consciousness scores.
