## Evidence distinctions

- **Cell identity:** marker, antibody, reporter या transcript से labeled/enriched population; perfect identity नहीं।
- **Morphology:** shape, process branching, density और tissue location।
- **Physiology:** calcium, membrane response, metabolism, uptake, phagocytosis या cell-cell communication under defined conditions।
- **Omics:** RNA, chromatin, protein, metabolite और lipid profiles; state signatures और associations।
- **Post-mortem:** preserved tissue में in-situ static snapshot।
- **PET:** selected molecular target पर radiotracer binding; limited cell specificity और tracer-dependent interpretation।
- **MRI:** tissue-level structural या hemodynamic contrast; glia-specific measure नहीं।
- **Models:** culture, iPSC, organoid और animal systems; human in-vivo equivalence automatically नहीं।

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose neurological or psychiatric disorders from glial markers, PET/MRI, fluid biomarkers, or research labels. Do not imply a single marker proves a cell’s full identity or that an animal, culture, or iPSC result automatically describes a human. Do not use “activated glia” as a universal binary state. No treatment, prescription, or personalized advice is included.
