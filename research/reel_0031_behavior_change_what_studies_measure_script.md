# Reel 0031 — behavior change: studies क्या मापते हैं?

## Angle

Behavior-change research separates intention and self-report from observed or recorded behavior, initial response from maintenance, and efficacy from real-world effectiveness.

## Hindi narration draft

क्या किसी का “मैं बदलूँगा” कहना behavior change का proof है? Research में intention और action अलग measures हैं।

Behavior-change study पहले target behavior और time window तय करती है। फिर देखती है: लोग क्या report करते हैं, क्या objectively record होता है, कितनी बार behavior हुआ, और क्या change follow-up में बना रहा।

Survey intention, attitude या confidence माप सकता है। लेकिन stated plan actual action नहीं। Observation, attendance record, device data, transaction या task performance अलग तरह का evidence दे सकते हैं। हर measure की अपनी limitation है।

Evaluation के भी अलग सवाल होते हैं। Efficacy पूछती है कि controlled conditions में intervention काम कर सकता है या नहीं। Effectiveness पूछती है कि real-world setting में क्या हुआ। Process research देखती है कि intervention वास्तव में deliver हुआ या नहीं, और mechanism study पूछती है कि change कैसे हुआ।

Short-term improvement को long-term maintenance मत समझिए। Adherence बीच में घट सकती है, barriers बदल सकते हैं, और अलग population या context में result अलग हो सकता है।

Study पढ़ते समय पूछिए: outcome self-report था या objective record? Follow-up कितना था? Comparison group और context क्या थे? Group average को personal guarantee मत समझिए। Behavior change research useful है, magic promise नहीं। यह education है, personal, medical या financial advice नहीं।

## Caption plan

Use 20–22 short Hindi captions. Keep “intention,” “self-report,” “objective record,” “efficacy,” “effectiveness,” “maintenance,” “adherence,” “follow-up,” and “context” readable. Cues must be ordered, non-overlapping, and end within the actual narration duration.

## Four-scene visual plan

1. **Plan versus action:** An anonymous checklist labeled intention leads to a separate observed-action record, with no individual score or judgment.
2. **Measurement methods:** Neutral icons represent survey, observation, device/record, task performance, and follow-up timeline.
3. **Evaluation questions:** Four panels show efficacy, real-world effectiveness, mechanism, and implementation/context as distinct research lenses.
4. **Maintenance guardrail:** A timeline shows initial change and later follow-up without a guaranteed upward arrow, ending with questions about outcome type, comparison, and context.

## Safety and disclosure constraints

Do not give health, financial, or personal behavior prescriptions. Avoid guarantees, manipulative persuasion, diagnosis, and individual predictions. Use “measured,” “reported,” “recorded,” “compared,” “average,” and “context-specific.” End with: AI-generated Hindi narration and deterministic conceptual visuals; educational content, not personal, medical, or financial advice.
