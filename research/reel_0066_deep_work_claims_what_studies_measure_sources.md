# Reel 0066 — गहरे काम के दावे: अध्ययन वास्तव में क्या मापते हैं

## Research question

जब कोई “deep work” या uninterrupted focus के फायदे का दावा करता है, तो studies वास्तव में कौन-सा task, outcome, interruption, subjective report, या neural correlate मापती हैं?

## Safe operational categories

**Sustained-attention performance:** accuracy, reaction time, omissions, false alarms, variability, and performance change across time-on-task in a defined vigilance or continuous-performance task.

**Interruption or switching outcome:** difference between uninterrupted and interrupted conditions, or between switch and repeat trials, under controlled timing and instructions.

**Subjective effort and concentration:** participant ratings of effort, fatigue, focus, or perceived productivity. These are self-reports and are not identical to objective performance.

**Real-world task outcome:** an observable outcome such as prescribing errors in a named clinical environment. It is ecologically relevant but still tied to the population, task, and design.

**Neural or physiological correlate:** brain or body signals associated with a task or reported state. Such signals are indirect, context-bound correlates rather than direct readings of thought or “depth.”

**Generalization:** “deep work” is a popular umbrella phrase, not one standardized laboratory variable. Evidence must specify whether the claim concerns simple vigilance, knowledge work, digital multitasking, interruptions, sleep, or another manipulation.

## Safe conclusion

Studies relevant to deep-work claims usually measure a defined task, time-on-task pattern, interruption or switching manipulation, behavioral outcome, self-report, or task-associated neural activity. These measurements can inform sustained attention, interference, workload, and performance under tested conditions. They do not directly measure a hidden universal state called “deep work,” prove uninterrupted work is always better for every task, or justify judging a person’s character or clinical status from one result.

## Safety and editorial boundaries

This is public education, not diagnosis, treatment, prescription, or individualized productivity advice. Do not claim that every notification causes the same loss, that one uninterrupted session guarantees superior output, or that a single vigilance or multitasking score defines a person’s intelligence, discipline, or health.
