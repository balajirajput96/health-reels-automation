# Reel 0030 browser findings

On 2026-08-25, two public sources were opened.

1. Decety, Bartal, Uzefovsky, and Knafo-Noam, “Empathy as a driver of prosocial behaviour: highly conserved neurobehavioural mechanisms across species” (*Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*, DOI 10.1098/rstb.2015.0077; PMC4685523), was read from public PMC full text. The review treats empathy as a heterogeneous concept and explicitly distinguishes sensitivity to others’ states from possible consequent behaviors such as helping or comforting. It also discusses behavioral, physiological, and neural mechanisms. The reel will therefore avoid equating a self-reported feeling of empathy with actual helping.

2. Thielmann, Böhm, Ott, and Hilbig, “Economic Games: An Introduction and Guide for Research” (*Collabra: Psychology*, 2021, DOI 10.1525/collabra.19004), was extracted from the public article page. The methods guide states that many disciplines use economic games to measure prosocial behavior in controlled experimental settings, and that game structure and situational affordances shape interpretation. The reel will use allocation, cooperation, and social-dilemma choices as task-specific measures, not as complete measures of character or real-world altruism.

The Collabra page displayed a captcha after extraction, but its citation, abstract, and methodological summary were available. No claim beyond the extracted article text was used.
