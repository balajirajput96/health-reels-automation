## Evidence distinctions

- **Autobiographical memory:** personally experienced past event का narrative, detail, specificity, vividness या source report।
- **Episodic future thinking:** possible personal future event का imagined description या rating।
- **Remember–know/source judgment:** उस task में contextual/source recollection और familiarity को अलग करने की procedure।
- **Behavioral proxy:** flexible choice, planning, or what–where–when performance, interpreted under task assumptions।
- **Subjective phenomenology:** vividness, reliving, emotional quality या first-person experience का self-report; direct recording नहीं।
- **Neural/physiological correlate:** defined remembering/imagining task के दौरान associated signal।
- **Interpretation limit:** memory report exact replay नहीं; future imagination prediction नहीं; behavior alone private experience सिद्ध नहीं करता।

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose memory loss, trauma, personality, or any mental disorder from a person’s memories, future imagination, or task performance. Do not claim remembered events are exact recordings or imagined events are predictions. Do not infer private consciousness from a behavioral or neural correlate. No personalized advice or treatment claim is included.
