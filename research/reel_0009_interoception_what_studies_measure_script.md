# Reel 0009 — इंटरोसेप्शन: अध्ययन वास्तव में क्या मापते हैं

## Unique angle

इंटरोसेप्शन को एक single “body-awareness score” मानने के बजाय दिखाना कि studies अलग-अलग चीज़ें माप सकती हैं: recorded bodily signal के साथ performance, अपने बारे में self-report, attention, और performance-confidence का correspondence.

## Evidence distinctions

- **Directly observed:** ECG या wearable से recorded cardiac signal, presented tones or timing, participant responses, heartbeat counts, confidence ratings, and questionnaire answers.
- **Task-derived:** performance/accuracy scores, correspondence indices, and metacognitive awareness estimates.
- **Self-report:** beliefs about bodily attention or accuracy; these are not interchangeable with objective performance.
- **Interpretation limit:** a task may be influenced by timing estimates, expectations, device choice, physiological state, and task design; no single score represents all interoception.

## Four-scene visual plan

### Scene 1 — Recorded signal and timed detection

Vertical conceptual motion graphic: a neutral silhouette beside a stylized ECG trace and two timing markers, with a clean “signal ↔ response” comparison. No clinical diagnosis, real patient, or unsupported number. On-screen text: “recorded signal + response”.

### Scene 2 — Heartbeat counting and confounds

Show a clock, heartbeat dots, and a counter card. A second translucent card labeled “estimate?” points to time and typical-rate cues, making clear that the task can involve non-interoceptive estimation. On-screen text: “count ≠ pure signal”.

### Scene 3 — Accuracy, self-report, and confidence

Three-panel explanatory layout: objective task performance, questionnaire/self-report, and confidence rating. Connectors are labeled “compare” and “report,” not “diagnose.” On-screen text: “accuracy / sensibility / confidence”.

### Scene 4 — Measurement guardrail

A checklist with three rows: “क्या record हुआ?”, “participant ने क्या किया?”, “score में क्या infer हुआ?” A final gauge remains contextual. Closing text: “एक score = पूरी interoception नहीं”. Include the visual-disclosure wording.

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose anxiety, depression, eating disorders, or any other condition from an interoception task. Do not claim that heartbeat counting reveals a person’s complete body awareness, emotion ability, or health. Do not present a questionnaire as an objective physiological measure. Do not promise that a task, wearable, or practice will improve health. Keep method-development findings and systematic-review conclusions distinct from causal or clinical claims.
