## Evidence distinctions

- **Theory/model:** hierarchical inference, generative models, prediction and prediction error।
- **Behavioral measure:** accuracy, reaction time, detection, learning, perceptual report, surprise response।
- **Stimulus manipulation:** expected, repeated, omitted, deviant, or statistically regular versus irregular input।
- **Neural measures:** EEG/MEG timing or power, fMRI BOLD, intracranial or single-unit signals, laminar recordings।
- **Connectivity:** functional correlations, phase coupling, directed or model-derived interactions।
- **Computational comparison:** Bayesian, predictive-coding, neural-network or language-model fits।
- **Interpretation limit:** no single measure uniquely proves prediction error, private prior, exact biological implementation, cause, diagnosis, or symptom explanation。

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not claim that predictive processing is proven as a complete theory of the brain, that a particular EEG/fMRI pattern is a unique prediction-error signal, or that an individual’s priors or symptoms can be read from a scan. Do not use the framework for diagnosis, treatment, prescription, or personalized prediction. No medical advice is included.
