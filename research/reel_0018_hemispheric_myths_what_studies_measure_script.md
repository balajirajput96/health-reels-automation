# Reel 0018 — मस्तिष्क-गोलार्ध मिथक: studies वास्तव में क्या मापते हैं?

## Unique angle

“Left-brained” या “right-brained” व्यक्ति कोई scan से निकला universal personality type नहीं है। Neuroscience में hemispheric lateralization कुछ defined functions, tasks और networks के लिए मापी जाती है; पूरे इंसान को दो brain labels में नहीं बाँटा जाता।

## Evidence distinctions

- **Functional lateralization:** किसी defined function या task में left-right activity difference।
- **Language production:** word generation, picture naming या speech task में measured laterality; पूरे cognition का synonym नहीं।
- **Visuospatial attention:** landmark या related task में measured laterality; creativity का direct measure नहीं।
- **Resting-state fMRI:** task के बिना BOLD time-series की statistical dependence; personality label का direct test नहीं।
- **Lateralized hubs/networks:** selected graph में अधिक lateralized connections वाले regions; global “hemisphere dominance” नहीं।
- **Laterality index:** chosen regions, task, preprocessing और contrast से निकला quantitative summary।
- **Individual inference:** group-level or sample-level results को किसी viewer पर लागू नहीं किया जा सकता बिना अलग validated evidence के।

## Four-scene visual plan

### Scene 1 — Task-specific asymmetry

A split visual shows a language task card and a visuospatial line-bisection card, with different hemispheric glow patterns. The composition makes clear that the tasks are separate measurements, not two personality boxes.

### Scene 2 — Resting-state connections

A calm brain silhouette is paired with two BOLD time-series traces and a network of local left/right connections. A bounded label indicates statistical dependence rather than a literal cable.

### Scene 3 — Hubs, networks, and analysis choices

A graph with selected hubs feeds through cards for atlas, preprocessing, and laterality index. The scene shows that the number is derived from choices and data, not a universal brain identity.

### Scene 4 — Myth versus measurement guardrail

A bright “defined function + defined task + defined method” panel is separated from crossed-out labels “creative right brain”, “logical left brain”, “personality score”, and “intelligence verdict”.

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose neurological or psychiatric disorders from laterality language. Do not infer intelligence, personality, talent, creativity, logic, or life outcomes from a hemisphere label, handedness, or a scan. Do not present group averages as individual facts. Do not claim that one hemisphere is inactive during tasks or that all cognition is divided into two sides. No treatment, prescription, or personalized advice is included.
