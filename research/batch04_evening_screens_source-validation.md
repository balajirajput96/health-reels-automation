# Batch 04 — Evening Screens, Light Exposure, and Sleep Wind-Down: Source Validation

**Topic status:** Eligible for a cautious, general-public education Reel. The Reel must discuss light exposure as one environmental timing cue, not as a clinical assessment, management, or assuranced sleep intervention.

## Validated framing

| Source | Supported educational point | Use in Reel |
|---|---|---|
| [Sleep Foundation — *Blue Light: What It Is and How It Affects Sleep*](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/bedroom-environment/blue-light) | Electronic screens and artificial lights can emit blue light. Light exposure can affect circadian timing, and blue light is described as having a strong circadian effect. | Explain that evening screen light is one cue the body may interpret as daytime-like alertness. |
| [Harvard Health — *Blue light has a dark side*](https://www.health.harvard.edu/healthy-aging-and-longevity/blue-light-has-a-dark-side) | Light at night can affect circadian rhythms; blue wavelengths are described as more disruptive at night than other wavelengths. | Use cautious wording: bright evening light and screens may make it harder for some people to transition toward sleep. |
| [National Sleep Foundation — *Good Light, Bad Light, and Better Sleep*](https://www.thensf.org/good-light-bad-light-and-better-sleep/) | Light exposure is an important factor in sleep-wake timing. The organization recommends lowering evening light and reducing screen exposure while winding down. | Present a calmer evening light environment as a routine cue that some people may choose to notice, not as personal professional guidance. |

## Approved claim boundary

> Research describes light exposure as an important cue for the body’s sleep-wake timing. Bright screens and other evening light can promote alertness and may make a sleep wind-down feel less natural for some people.

The Reel must not claim that screens cause insomnia, promise that screen reduction improves sleep, prescribe an exact screen cutoff, discuss management, or give personalized advice. It must retain the final spoken and caption disclaimer: **“यह Reel general education के लिए है, personal professional guidance नहीं। यदि sleep concerns daily life को affect कर रहे हों, तो qualified professional से बात करें।”**
