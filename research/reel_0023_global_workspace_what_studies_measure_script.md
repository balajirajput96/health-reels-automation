## Evidence distinctions

- **Theory:** global broadcasting और nonlinear recurrent amplification एक hypothesis है।
- **Reportability:** verbal report, button press, visibility या confidence rating।
- **Behavioral access:** accuracy, d-prime, reaction time, working-memory या task performance।
- **Neural correlates:** EEG/ERP, MEG, fMRI, intracranial voltage, spectral power, synchrony और decoding।
- **Confounds:** attention, report, decision, working memory और task relevance signals को प्रभावित कर सकते हैं।
- **Adversarial testing:** preregistered predictions, multimodal measurement और replication theory claims को constrain करते हैं।
- **Interpretation limit:** correlate, model fit या activation अपने-आप cause, personal consciousness score या diagnosis नहीं।

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not claim that GNW is proven, that one region or single ERP causes consciousness, or that a scan can read a person’s private experience. Do not present model predictions as direct measurements. Do not make individual clinical, mental-health, intelligence, or personality inferences from workspace-related signals. No treatment, prescription, or personalized advice is included.
