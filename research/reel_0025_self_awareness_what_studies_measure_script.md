## Evidence distinctions

- **Construct:** self-awareness multidimensional है, universal single variable नहीं।
- **Self-report:** questionnaires reported self-focused attention, self-knowledge, reflective experience।
- **Metacognition:** confidence, error awareness, calibration, confidence–accuracy relation।
- **Self-recognition:** mirror/photo/video/body-action recognition behavior।
- **Bodily self:** ownership, agency, perspective, multisensory illusion, interoceptive or sensorimotor measures।
- **Social/implicit:** self-reference effect, action attribution, observer or implicit associations।
- **Neural correlates:** EEG/ERP, fMRI, connectivity, stimulation or autonomic physiology।
- **Interpretation limit:** no single measure proves complete self-awareness, consciousness, personality, identity, causation, or diagnosis।
