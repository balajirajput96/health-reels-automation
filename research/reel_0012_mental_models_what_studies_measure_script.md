# Reel 0012 — मानसिक मॉडल: studies क्या measure करती हैं?

## Unique angle

Mental model को “दिमाग में बनी पूरी तस्वीर” मानने के बजाय दिखाना कि research controlled reasoning performance, explanations, predictions, surveys, interviews, card sorts और concept maps से अलग-अलग हिस्सों का अनुमान लगाती है। हर method का inference अलग होता है।

“इसलिए सही सवाल है: कौन-सा task था, क्या observe हुआ, और उससे कितना inference justified है? Mental model कोई universal score नहीं, बल्कि evidence से बनाया गया bounded explanation है। यह educational reel है; diagnosis या treatment advice नहीं। Visuals conceptual हैं, literal mind-reading नहीं।”

## Evidence distinctions

- **Theory:** a mental model is a theoretical representation of relationships, mechanisms, expectations, or causal beliefs.
- **Behavioral proxy:** accuracy, errors, and reaction time on controlled reasoning problems.
- **Elicitation proxy:** surveys, forced choices, interviews, card sorts, cognitive task analysis, and concept maps.
- **Interpretation limit:** the observed response is not identical to the full internal representation; language, memory, strategy, and context can influence the result.

## Four-scene visual plan

### Scene 1 — Reasoning task

A vertical conceptual study board shows two linked premise cards feeding into a conclusion card, with a split path for “follows?” and “does not follow?”. A small stopwatch and checkmark represent reaction time and accuracy. No fabricated participant data or numerical result is shown.

### Scene 2 — Task properties

A set of modular cards changes from two premises to three, from one relation to two, and from a simple relation to a more complex one. The scene communicates that researchers manipulate stimulus properties and compare difficulty, not that one task is a complete intelligence test.

### Scene 3 — Elicitation methods

Four clean panels show a survey scale, a speech bubble for an interview, sorted concept cards, and a node-link concept map. An abstract transparent overlay connects them but remains incomplete, illustrating that each method samples a different part of a model.

### Scene 4 — Inference guardrail

A funnel receives “task response”, “explanation”, and “prediction”, then exits as a modest “bounded interpretation” rather than a brain scan or mind-reading image. End with a three-question checklist: task, observation, inference.

## Safety and evidence boundary

Do not diagnose learning disorders, intelligence, personality, or clinical conditions from a reasoning task, interview, survey, or concept map. Do not claim that mental models are always visual, fully accessible to introspection, or accurately captured by one score. Do not claim that one method proves a causal model of behavior or that training a model guarantees real-world outcomes.
