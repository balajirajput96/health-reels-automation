# Reel 0026 — बदली हुई चेतना: studies वास्तव में क्या मापते हैं?

## Angle

A changed state of consciousness is not one universal “level.” Research separates the person’s reported phenomenology from observable behavior, physiology, brain signals, and model-derived summaries.

## Hindi narration draft

क्या altered state of consciousness को एक single score से मापा जा सकता है? Research का practical जवाब है—नहीं।

पहली layer है phenomenology: व्यक्ति बाद में questionnaire में बताता है कि perception, time, body, emotion, thought या self-experience कैसे बदले। OAV, 5D-ASC, PCI, HRS और MEQ30 जैसे instruments अलग-अलग dimensions पूछते हैं।

दूसरी layer है behavior: response, attention या task performance में क्या बदलाव आया? तीसरी layer physiology और brain signals की है—EEG, MEG, fMRI, blood flow, connectivity या complexity measures।

कुछ studies subjective reports को इन signals से relate करती हैं। लेकिन relation, direct mind-reading नहीं है। और questionnaire का factor biological module या universal truth नहीं माना जा सकता।

इसलिए study पढ़ते समय पूछिए: state कैसे induce हुई, measure कब लिया गया, instrument ने कौन-सा अनुभव define किया, और result correlation है या causal claim? Altered states research useful है, पर interpretation हमेशा method और limits के साथ होनी चाहिए। यह education है—diagnosis, treatment या substance advice नहीं।

## Caption plan

Use 20–22 short Hindi captions. Keep English instrument names as written, split long sentences, and ensure the final disclaimer has a positive nonzero interval ending within the actual narration duration.

## Four-scene visual plan

1. **Multidimensional state map:** An abstract human silhouette surrounded by distinct, non-literal rings for perception, time, body, emotion, thought, and self-experience; no private-thought visualization or clinical label.
2. **Questionnaire and behavior:** A clean research desk with anonymous response cards, sliders, and a separate task-response path; no identifiable participant, score diagnosis, or substance depiction.
3. **Signals and correlation:** EEG-like waveform, fMRI-style network nodes, and physiological pulse line converging toward a comparison panel; use visual connectors, not claims of reading thoughts.
4. **Methods-and-limits compass:** A balanced scientific scale showing “measure,” “correlation,” and “interpretation,” ending on a calm educational frame with no treatment or use instructions.

## Safety and disclosure constraints

Do not recommend or instruct on psychoactive substances, breathing practices, fasting, meditation intensity, hypnosis, or sensory deprivation. Do not equate altered states with illness, spiritual attainment, intelligence, identity, or guaranteed benefit. Do not diagnose or infer a viewer’s experience. Say “reported,” “associated,” “measured,” or “modeled” where appropriate. End with: AI-generated Hindi narration and deterministic conceptual visuals; educational content, not medical advice.
