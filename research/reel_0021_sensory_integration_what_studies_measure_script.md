## Evidence distinctions

- **Psychophysics:** detection, discrimination, accuracy, threshold, reaction time और response bias।
- **Cue weighting:** अलग reliability वाले cues में behavioral bias और precision।
- **Temporal binding:** subjective timing estimates, estimation error और temporal-binding window।
- **Perceptual illusions:** audiovisual या multisensory conflict में reported perceptual change।
- **Electrophysiology:** single-unit firing, EEG/ERP timing और neural tuning।
- **Neuroimaging:** fMRI, PET, MEG या related regional/network correlates।
- **Computational models:** Bayesian causal inference या reliability-weighted model का fit।
- **Interpretation limit:** Behavioral effect, neural correlate और theory अलग evidence categories हैं।
