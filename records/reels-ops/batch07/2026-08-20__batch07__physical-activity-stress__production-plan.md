## Claim Boundary

Physical activity is framed as one component of a broader health routine. The Reel may say public-health sources discuss activity alongside brain health and emotional balance, and that large research syntheses have studied psychological-distress outcomes. It must not diagnose stress or a mental-health condition, prescribe an activity plan, promise stress relief, or describe activity as replacement for professional care.

## Global Creative Definition

A calm realistic editorial visual system shows one adult South Asian person in their early 30s, warm medium-brown skin, dark wavy hair, wearing a soft rust overshirt over a cream T-shirt and dark trousers. The person moves through an everyday urban park and a warm home-office setting, with gentle morning daylight, muted terracotta, sage green, cream, and slate colors. The person is never depicted as distressed, “fixed,” treated, or athletic; movement is ordinary and self-paced. No readable text, logos, labels, health charts, branded apparel, fitness trackers, medical imagery, watermarks, or on-screen claims.
