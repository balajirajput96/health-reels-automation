## Claim Boundary

The Reel defines social connection as more than the number of people someone knows: quality, support, belonging, and the type of relationship matter. It may state that public-health sources and longitudinal research link supportive connection with health and wellbeing. It must not diagnose loneliness, promise that reaching out improves health or mood, blame people for isolation, or prescribe a social routine.

## Global Creative Definition

Calm realistic editorial B-roll follows two adult South Asian friends in their early 30s. One wears a muted teal overshirt and cream T-shirt; the other wears a warm clay cardigan and off-white top. Scenes alternate between a quiet neighborhood café exterior, a public park bench, and a softly lit home dining table. The tone is warm but restrained: voluntary, ordinary friendly connection, not romance, therapy, crisis intervention, or dramatic transformation. Muted teal, clay, cream, warm wood, and soft late-afternoon daylight; no readable text, logos, labels, phone screens, medical imagery, health charts, watermarks, or on-screen claims.
