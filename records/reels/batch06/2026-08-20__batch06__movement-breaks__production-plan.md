## Claim Boundary

The Reel explains that interrupting long sitting with short movement is one way to reduce sedentary time and that attention-related research is mixed. It may mention that particular studies found favorable attention or reaction-time measures under particular protocols. It must not promise focus, prescribe a break schedule, diagnose attention difficulty, or present a small laboratory study as a personal recommendation.

## Global Creative Definition

The visual system is calm, realistic editorial B-roll with an adult South Asian office worker in a muted sage overshirt over an off-white T-shirt. The setting is a bright but soft contemporary workspace: light oak desk, unbranded laptop, notebook, water glass, indoor plant, and cool daylight with gentle warm practical accents. Camera movement stays physically plausible and slow. Every visual remains unbranded and contains no health charts, medical imagery, logos, captions, watermarks, labels, or textual claims. No embedded speech, sound effect, or music is required.
