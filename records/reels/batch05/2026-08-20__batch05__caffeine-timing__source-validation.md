## Approved Claim Boundary

> Caffeine can come from several foods and drinks, and its effects can vary. Evidence suggests that a substantial caffeine dose taken even hours before bedtime may affect sleep for some people. This Reel is about noticing timing and label information, not prescribing a personal cutoff or dose.

## Do Not Claim

- Do not state that one fixed caffeine cutoff works for everyone.
- Do not prescribe milligram targets, calculate dose, or advise medication changes.
- Do not imply caffeine is always harmful or that it explains any viewer’s sleep difficulty.
- Do not make claims about addiction, anxiety, heart conditions, pregnancy, children, or clinical insomnia beyond the cited general education boundary.
