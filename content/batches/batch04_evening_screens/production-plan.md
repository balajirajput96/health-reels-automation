**Music:** None. Keep generated clip audio unobtrusive beneath narration.

## Claim boundary

The Reel describes evening light as one environmental timing cue. It does not diagnose a sleep disorder, claim that screens cause insomnia, promise a sleep outcome, prescribe a screen cutoff, or give individualized advice.

## Visual consistency rules

Use a realistic calm editorial style, warm-to-neutral evening palette, uncluttered home interior, and varied adult figures with no identifiable real person. All images and clips must be vertical, upright, free of text, logos, watermarks, UI, clinical imagery, or product placement. The primary reference image is mandatory before generating motion clips. Each motion prompt must include a detailed transition description, preserve the presence of key objects throughout the clip, and end with **“No background music.”**

## Subtitle rule

Create subtitles only after transcribing the actual assembled narration. Use that transcript rather than the written script as the subtitle source.

## Final validation gate

Before any Instagram upload, verify: 60-second export; portrait geometry; decodable H.264/AAC file; audible narration; source-derived cover; audio-faithful subtitles if used; complete neutral caption and disclaimer; correct account; AI-content disclosure; and no duplicate final asset or post ID.
