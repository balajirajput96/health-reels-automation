# REEL-0036 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining "Implementation Intentions" (If-Then Planning)—how explicitly linking a specific situational cue to a targeted behavioral response bridges the intention-action gap and speeds cognitive cue detection.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults looking to translate abstract goals into actionable daily routines. The takeaway is that pre-deciding "when and where" an action occurs increases the likelihood of execution without relying solely on momentary memory.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why broad goals ("I will exercise") often fail compared to specific conditional plans.
2. Goal Intention vs Implementation Intention: distinguishing vague desire from If-Then pairing.
3. Cognitive Mechanism: heightened perceptual readiness and faster cue detection in working memory.
4. Behavioral Transfer: automatic initiation of the targeted action upon encountering the cue.
5. Practice: formulating simple If-Then statements for daily routines ("If it is 7 PM, then I walk 15 mins").
6. Nuance: acknowledging that obstacles, motivation, and context modify effectiveness over time.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is behavioral science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic If-Then conditional decision branches and calendar cues, lower-third caption-safe area, and no self-help hyperbole.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute behavioral science to Peter Gollwitzer's meta-analyses and experimental trials on implementation intentions. Use "may increase likelihood" and "cue-response link." Do not claim If-Then planning guarantees instant behavior change.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
