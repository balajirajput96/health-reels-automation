# REEL-0075 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining that personally enjoyable and meaningful leisure activities may serve as optional recovery cues after stressful days. It distinguishes observational associations from causal claims and avoids turning hobbies into a productivity or medical prescription.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults looking for realistic recovery ideas. The takeaway is to choose a small, accessible activity that feels genuinely meaningful and does not become another obligation. The reel should communicate that no single hobby is best for everyone.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals animated with deterministic motion if official visual quotas permit. Native video generation is optional and may be used only if its official quota is available. If image generation remains exhausted, do not use unrelated visuals; record a blocked preproduction state.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: an adult arrives home after a demanding day and transitions into a calm, ordinary hobby space without a dramatic transformation.
2. Research frame: inclusive montage of reading, nature, music, craft, gardening, and quiet social connection; no text-bearing AI graphics.
3. Association: calm editorial research desk with abstract charts that contain no numbers, labels, or fake study screenshots.
4. Causality boundary: split visual language showing association as a connection, not an arrow of proven cause; use no medical biomarker imagery.
5. Meaning and choice: the person considers a few accessible activities and chooses one that fits their interests and circumstances.
6. Avoid obligation: a small, low-pressure hobby moment with realistic interruptions and no productivity timer, streak counter, or “optimize your life” message.
7. Safety: the person keeps the hobby in perspective and acknowledges professional support for persistent stress or serious health concerns; no crisis imagery.

## Visual constraints

Use a consistent realistic editorial documentary style with warm natural light, diverse adult representation, varied ability and access considerations, uncluttered environments, lower-third caption-safe space, and no logos, fake data, medical claims, spiritual certainty, or text-bearing AI graphics. Do not portray a hobby as a guaranteed reset, cure, or luxury lifestyle.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, use readable multiline blocks, remain bottom-centered, and avoid adding fixed durations, productivity promises, biomarker claims, or “best hobby” advice absent from the script.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute the observational leisure associations to Pressman et al. and the meaningful-participation survey association to Haim-Litevsky et al. Use “associated with” and “may,” not causal language. Do not claim hobbies lower cortisol, blood pressure, depression, or disease risk. Persistent stress and serious health concerns require qualified professional support; hobbies are not treatment.

## Disclosure

If produced, state that visuals are AI-generated reference images animated with deterministic motion. Record any official quota limitation. The package remains unpublished until separate editorial review.
