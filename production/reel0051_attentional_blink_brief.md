# REEL-0051 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining "Attentional Blink and Visual Processing Dynamics"—how the brain temporarily "blinks" its conscious awareness for 200–500 milliseconds after detecting a visual target in a rapid stream (RSVP), rendering a second target temporarily invisible while the first is consolidated into working memory. It attributes findings to Jane Raymond, Kimron Shapiro, and Marvin Chun.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults interested in cognitive psychology and visual attention limits. The takeaway is that visual attention has a temporal bottleneck: detecting one stimulus momentarily blinds us to immediately following events during the memory consolidation window.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why looking at rapid images causes you to completely miss the second picture right after spotting the first.
2. The RSVP Experiment: Rapid Serial Visual Presentation stream showing target T1 and target T2.
3. The Attentional Blink Window: the 200–500ms refractory period where T2 detection drops sharply.
4. Working Memory Bottleneck: the brain locking resources to encode T1 before it can register T2.
5. Lag-One Sparing: why a target appearing immediately (within 100ms) can sneak through before the gate closes.
6. Real-World Applications: driving, gaming, and rapid scanning where temporal awareness gaps occur.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is cognitive attention science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic RSVP stream cards (T1 detected -> Attentional gate closes / timer icon 200-500ms -> T2 missed), lower-third caption-safe area, and no ADHD diagnosis.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive science to peer-reviewed attentional blink literature (Raymond et al., Chun & Potter, Shapiro). Use "attentional blink," "temporal bottleneck," and "RSVP stream." Do not diagnose visual attention disorders.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
