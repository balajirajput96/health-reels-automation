# REEL-0136 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Neuroscience of Cold Water Immersion: Sustained 250% Dopamine Elevation without Crash, Noradrenaline Shock, and The 11-Minute Weekly Søberg Threshold"—how 2 to 3 minutes of cold water immersion (below 15°C) triggers a steady 2.5x surge in baseline dopamine that lasts for 3 to 4 hours without the steep rebound crash associated with sugar or digital stimulants. It attributes findings to Dr. Susanna Søberg and European Journal of Applied Physiology.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone suffering from low motivation, brain fog, or caffeine dependency. The takeaway is that brief cold exposure builds a long-lasting, crash-free neurochemical fountain of dopamine and mental resilience.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why taking a 2-minute cold shower gives you 4 hours of intense laser focus without any caffeine crash.
2. The 250% Dopamine Surge: European Journal of Applied Physiology showing cold water driving dopamine up by 250% and noradrenaline by 530%.
3. Crash-Free Elevation: unlike addictive stimulants that cause dopamine crashes, cold-induced dopamine stays elevated smoothly for 3 to 4 hours.
4. Brown Adipose Tissue (BAT) Activation: thermogenic uncoupling protein 1 (UCP-1) firing up metabolism and glucose burning.
5. The Søberg Threshold: 11 minutes of cold exposure distributed across an entire week is all that's required for long-term adaptation.
6. The Cold Shower Protocol: end your morning warm shower with 60 to 90 seconds of pure cold water on your neck and back.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute thermal neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Søberg, Šrámek, Huberman). Use "Cold Immersion Dopamine," "250% Sustained Elevation," "Søberg 11-Minute Threshold," and "Noradrenaline Surge."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
