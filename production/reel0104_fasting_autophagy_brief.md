# REEL-0104 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Intermittent Fasting & Autophagy: Cellular Recycling, Yoshinori Ohsumi Nobel Discovery, mTOR Downregulation, and AMPK Activation"—how giving the digestive system an extended break triggers cellular self-cleaning (autophagy) where dysfunctional organelles, misfolded proteins, and senescent components are broken down and recycled into pristine cellular energy. It attributes findings to Yoshinori Ohsumi, Satchin Panda, and cellular biologists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking longevity, metabolic flexibility, and cellular health. The takeaway is that fasting is not about starvation—it is an evolutionary recycling switch where cells shift from growth (mTOR) to repair and cleanup (autophagy and AMPK).

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why stopping eating for 14-16 hours triggers your body's built-in vacuum cleaner to consume damaged cells and toxic waste.
2. The Nobel Prize Discovery: Yoshinori Ohsumi winning the 2016 Nobel Prize in Medicine for unraveling the biological mechanisms of autophagy (self-eating).
3. The mTOR vs AMPK Switch: constant feeding keeping mTOR (growth) active; fasting depleting nutrients to activate AMPK (energy sensor) and trigger cleanup.
4. Lysosomal Waste Digestion: cells forming double-membrane autophagosomes that fuse with lysosomes to dissolve junk proteins into fresh amino acids.
5. Brain & Mitochondrial Renewal: autophagy removing damaged mitochondria (mitophagy) in neurons, reducing neurodegenerative plaque risks.
6. The 16:8 Window Protocol: practical intermittent fasting with an 8-hour feeding window and 16 hours of digestive rest and hydration.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure (pregnant women and diabetics must consult doctors).

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute autophagy research to peer-reviewed literature (Ohsumi, Panda, Longo, Mattson). Use "Autophagy Nobel Prize," "mTOR vs AMPK switch," "mitochondrial renewal," and "time-restricted feeding." Include appropriate safety notices.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
