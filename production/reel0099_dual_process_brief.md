# REEL-0099 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Dual-Process Theory: System 1 (Fast, Automatic Intuition) vs System 2 (Slow, Deliberate Calculation)"—how the human mind operates through two distinct cognitive modes, why System 1 relies on heuristics and causes cognitive biases, and how deliberately engaging System 2 prevents impulse errors and bad decisions. It attributes findings to Daniel Kahneman, Amos Tversky, and cognitive psychologists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone wanting to make wiser financial, personal, and professional decisions. The takeaway is that System 1 handles 95% of daily tasks automatically, but major decisions require stepping back to consciously engage System 2 to override gut assumptions.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why you instantly know the answer to 2+2, but have to stop, breathe, and think to calculate 17×24.
2. Kahneman's Dual-Process Model: System 1 (fast, emotional, automatic) and System 2 (slow, logical, effortful).
3. System 1 Energy Efficiency: System 1 operating with minimal energy to keep us alive and make split-second daily choices.
4. The Heuristic Trap: System 1 using cognitive shortcuts that lead to impulsive purchases, anger, and cognitive biases.
5. System 2 Deliberate Override: System 2 requiring prefrontal glucose and effort to analyze facts, double-check logic, and override System 1.
6. Practical Decision Rule: pausing 10 seconds during high-stakes choices to switch execution from System 1 to System 2.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute dual-process theory to peer-reviewed literature (Kahneman, Tversky, Stanovich, Evans). Use "Dual-Process Theory," "System 1 vs System 2," "cognitive heuristics," and "deliberate reasoning." Do not diagnose cognitive impairments.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
