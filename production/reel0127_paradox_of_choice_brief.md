# REEL-0127 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Paradox of Choice & Decision Fatigue: Prefrontal Glucose Depletion, Analysis Paralysis, and Steve Jobs' Uniform Secret"—how facing excessive options depletes prefrontal executive energy, increases post-decision regret, and leads to inaction. It attributes findings to Dr. Barry Schwartz and Dr. Sheena Iyengar (the Jam Experiment).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is professionals, shoppers, content consumers, and over-thinkers who feel exhausted by late afternoon despite doing no physical work. The takeaway is that more choices lead to less happiness; ruthless simplification and automation of trivial decisions preserves cognitive horsepower for high-leverage tasks.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why looking at a menu with 100 dishes makes you feel exhausted and unhappy with whatever you finally order.
2. The Famous Jam Experiment: Sheena Iyengar showing 24 jam choices attracted lookers but made only 3% buy, while 6 choices resulted in 30% buying.
3. Barry Schwartz's Paradox of Choice: more choices causing cognitive friction, FOMO, self-blame, and analysis paralysis.
4. Prefrontal Decision Fatigue: every minor micro-choice (what to wear, what to eat) depleting cellular glucose and prefrontal bandwidth.
5. The High-Performer Hack: why Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg, and Barack Obama wear identical minimalist outfits to eliminate morning decision fatigue.
6. The 3-Option Rule: limit everyday decisions to 3 options maximum and automate your recurring breakfasts and wardrobes.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute decision science to peer-reviewed literature (Schwartz, Iyengar, Baumeister, Vohs). Use "Paradox of Choice," "Decision Fatigue," "Prefrontal Depletion," and "Analysis Paralysis."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
