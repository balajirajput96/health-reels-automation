# REEL-0027 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring social norms in behavioral science—specifically differentiating between descriptive norms (what most people actually do) and injunctive norms (what people ought to do)—and how environmental cues guide daily social behaviors.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults interested in social psychology and behavioral design. The takeaway is that subtle descriptive cues about collective behavior powerfully guide individual actions.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: how the visible actions of others shape our everyday decisions without words.
2. Evidence: social psychology research on descriptive vs injunctive norms.
3. Mechanism: descriptive cues acting as fast cognitive heuristics in unfamiliar situations.
4. Real-world example: towel reuse or energy conservation studies driven by peer norm cues.
5. Environmental design: placing positive normative messages to encourage prosocial habits.
6. Contextual nuance: recognizing cultural and individual differences in social conformity.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is social science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, minimalist social interaction diagrams and environment signage graphics, lower-third caption-safe area, and no manipulative marketing claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute behavioral science to peer-reviewed social norms research (Cialdini et al.). Use "may influence" and "normative cues." Do not present social norms as manipulation techniques.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
