# REEL-0019 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining "social jet-lag"—the discrepancy between weekday alarm-driven schedules and weekend sleep-in patterns—and its effect on circadian rhythm alignment and Monday morning fatigue. It attributes findings to peer-reviewed sleep research in PMC and the American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults experiencing sluggishness on Monday mornings or erratic weekend sleep patterns. The takeaway is that minimizing large shifts between weekday and weekend sleep times helps maintain a steady circadian rhythm.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why waking up on Monday feels so difficult after sleeping in late on weekends.
2. Evidence: sleep science definitions of social jetlag from PMC and AASM.
3. Mechanism: mismatch between biological clock and changing social sleep routines.
4. Impact: shifts leading to daytime sluggishness and circadian misalignment.
5. Support: keeping weekend sleep and wake times relatively consistent.
6. Feasibility: gradual small adjustments to bridge the schedule gap.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is not medical advice.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, minimalist sleep and clock visuals (comparing weekday vs weekend alarms), lower-third caption-safe area, and no claims to cure clinical sleep disorders.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute sleep research to PMC and AASM. Use "may help" and "supportive." Do not claim sleep schedule adjustments cure chronic insomnia or sleep apnea.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
