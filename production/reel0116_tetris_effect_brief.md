# REEL-0116 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Tetris Effect: Cognitive Priming, Involuntary Imagery, Neuroplastic Mindsets, and Reshaping Your Mental Filters"—how repetitive mental engagement with a specific activity physically primes your visual cortex and subconscious mind to perceive the world through that exact same pattern (e.g. seeing falling blocks everywhere, or seeing gratitude/opportunity vs threats). It attributes findings to Dr. Robert Stickgold, Shawn Achor, and cognitive neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone wanting to break negative thought spirals and cultivate an instinctive opportunistic mindset. The takeaway is that what you repeatedly feed your brain becomes its default perceptual filter—train your brain on positive pattern recognition.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why playing a game for hours makes you see blocks falling from the ceiling when you close your eyes at night.
2. Harvard's Tetris Experiment: Robert Stickgold discovering that repetitive gameplay creates hypnagogic imagery and alters subconscious pattern recognition.
3. The Cognitive Priming Mechanism: repeated neural firing creating hyper-sensitive perceptual filters in the visual and associative cortices.
4. The Negative Tetris Effect: scanning all day for mistakes, taxes, or threats making the brain scan your relationships and life for flaws automatically.
5. The Positive Tetris Effect: training the brain to scan for 3 positive things daily physically rewiring it to spot opportunities effortlessly.
6. Daily Mindset Protocol: writing down 3 specific things you are grateful for before bed to prime your night-time memory consolidation.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive priming neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Stickgold, Achor, Malpass, Posner). Use "Tetris Effect," "Cognitive Priming," "Perceptual Filter," and "Hypnagogic Consolidation."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
