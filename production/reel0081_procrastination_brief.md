# REEL-0081 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Procrastination: The Neuropsychology of Temporal Discounting and Why the Brain Prefers Now Over Later"—how the limbic system's preference for immediate rewards (dopamine-driven temporal discounting) systematically overrides the prefrontal cortex's long-term planning, creating the chronic avoidance loop known as procrastination. It attributes findings to Piers Steel, Fuschia Sirois, and behavioral procrastination researchers.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is students, knowledge workers, and creatives who struggle with chronic task avoidance. The takeaway is that procrastination is not laziness but a failure of emotional regulation—specifically, avoiding the aversive feelings (anxiety, boredom, self-doubt) triggered by difficult tasks—and that structured implementation intentions and the 2-minute rule can break the avoidance loop.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why the most productive people also procrastinate—and why willpower alone never fixes it.
2. The Steel Equation: Piers Steel's Procrastination Equation (Motivation = Expectancy × Value / Impulsiveness × Delay).
3. Temporal Discounting: the limbic system making the Netflix reward feel 10x bigger than tomorrow's deadline reward right now.
4. The Emotional Avoidance Loop: the core of procrastination is avoiding negative feelings (anxiety, self-doubt), not the task itself.
5. The Prefrontal Override: why the Prefrontal Cortex loses the battle against the limbic system when emotion is intense.
6. Implementation Intentions and 2-Minute Rule: "I will [action] at [time] in [place]" plus the 2-minute start rule.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic Temporal Discounting curves (Present Bias spike vs Future Value plateau), lower-third caption-safe area, and no psychiatric ADD/ADHD diagnosis claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute procrastination research to Steel, Sirois, Baumeister, Clear. Use "temporal discounting," "procrastination equation," "emotional avoidance loop," and "implementation intentions." Do not diagnose ADHD.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
