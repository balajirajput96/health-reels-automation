# REEL-0042 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring "Contemplative Practices: Categorizing Meditation Traditions"—comparing Focused Attention (Shamatha), Open Monitoring (Vipassana), and Compassion (Metta/Karuna) practices across cognitive mechanisms, attentional networks, and empirical outcomes.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking a structured scientific classification of different meditation styles. The takeaway is that distinct contemplative techniques cultivate different attentional and affective networks rather than a generic relaxation state.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why treating all meditation as "closing eyes and sitting quietly" misses the scientific diversity.
2. Focused Attention: top-down cognitive control sustaining attention on a single object (e.g., breath).
3. Open Monitoring: non-reactive sensory monitoring of thoughts and sensations without attachment.
4. Compassion / Prosocial: active generation of benevolence engaging caregiving and affective circuits.
5. Distinct Neural Profiles: differentiating dorsal attention network from default mode network modulation.
6. Matching Intentions: selecting the specific contemplative technique aligned with individual goals.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is cognitive contemplative science.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic three-way classification diagrams (Focus, Open Monitoring, Compassion), lower-third caption-safe area, and no religious dogma.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute contemplative science to peer-reviewed taxonomies (Lutz et al., Dahl et al.). Use "focused attention," "open monitoring," and "neural network engagement." Do not make absolute health claims.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
