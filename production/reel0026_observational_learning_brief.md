# REEL-0026 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining how observational learning is distinct from blind imitation. Cognitive science experiments measure how individuals observe both actions and outcomes, update internal prediction models, and apply learned associations to novel choices.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults interested in learning science and cognitive psychology. The takeaway is that observational learning involves extracting action-outcome information to update predictive models rather than simple copying.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why observational learning is more complex than simple imitation.
2. Observation layers: noticing the specific action vs observing its resulting outcome.
3. Behavioral metrics: measuring transfer to new situations, response accuracy, and reaction speed.
4. Computational modeling: extracting prediction errors and learning rates from choice data.
5. Neural correlates: using EEG and fMRI to track task-related processing without mind-reading.
6. Guardrails: distinguishing between copying, learning, and deep conceptual understanding.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is cognitive science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, abstract diagrammatic visuals showing observer-actor relationships, prediction error gauges, and response panels; lower-third caption-safe area; and no psychological diagnostic claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive science to peer-reviewed observational learning and reinforcement modeling studies. Use "task-specific associations" and "model estimates." Do not claim observational tasks evaluate global intelligence or personality.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
