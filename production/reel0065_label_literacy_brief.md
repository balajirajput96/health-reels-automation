# REEL-0065 production brief

## Identity and lifecycle

- Reel identity: `REEL-0065`
- Source ID: `reel_0065_reading_food_labels_added_sugars_decision_awareness`
- Working title: **Food Label पढ़ते समय सबसे पहले क्या देखें? Serving Size और Added Sugars**
- Target: Hindi-speaking adults; everyday food-choice and label-literacy education.
- Format: 9:16 portrait, approximately 60 seconds, Hindi narration, Hindi captions, no publication until package QC and Drive re-list verification pass.
- Evidence class: established FDA/CDC public-health label guidance.
- AI disclosure: visuals are AI-assisted conceptual illustrations or deterministic motion graphics; narration is AI-generated Hindi speech if synthetic voice is used.

## Editorial promise

The reel teaches a simple label-reading sequence: serving size, servings per container, total versus added sugars, and %DV. It does not prescribe a personal portion, claim that any product is inherently unhealthy, or promise weight loss or disease prevention.

## Seven-span timing plan

| Span | Approx. purpose | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook and serving-size focus | Close-up of a generic packaged-food label; serving-size row receives a clean highlight. |
| 2 | Serving size is a reference, not a recommendation | Split-screen conceptual comparison of a label card and a plate; no numeric medical claims. |
| 3 | Servings per container | One package becomes two clearly separated serving cards; show arithmetic concept without implying a viewer’s intake. |
| 4 | Total sugars versus added sugars | Two-lane visual: fruit/milk icons for naturally occurring sugars and processing/syrup icons for added sugars. |
| 5 | Read the Added Sugars line | Generic label close-up with the Added Sugars row highlighted; no brand logos or fabricated product claims. |
| 6 | %DV and 5%/20% guide | Clean vertical gauge labeled 0–100% DV with low/high zones; state it is a general guide and compare equal serving sizes. |
| 7 | 50 g reference and safety boundary | Calm summary card with “2,000-calorie reference diet” and “general education, not personal prescription”; end on the four-step checklist. |

## Visual constraints

Use topic-specific label close-ups, serving cards, sugar-source icons, and a neutral kitchen or grocery context. Avoid real brands, misleading health halos, sensational sugar imagery, diagnostic cues, or text-heavy AI-generated infographics. If images are generated, use 9:16 upright references and preserve readable, deterministic Hindi captions in post-production rather than relying on generated text for exact label wording.

## Audio and caption constraints

Use one consistent Hindi narrator profile and generate narration per span or approved span group. Captions must match the spoken Hindi, remain inside the 9:16 safe area, and be checked against the final audio. Any English terms retained in the narration—Serving Size, Added Sugars, and %DV—must remain consistent across script, captions, metadata, and on-screen labels.

## QC gates

1. Research sources and evidence boundaries are present.
2. Script preserves total-sugars versus added-sugars distinction.
3. Video is upright 720x1280 or better, H.264/AAC, approximately 60 seconds.
4. Hindi narration and captions are present and synchronized.
5. AI-assisted visual disclosure is included in metadata.
6. No personal diet or medical advice is presented.
7. Drive package includes video, narration, captions, research, brief, script, manifest, QC, and visual provenance.
8. Drive upload is re-listed from the exact reel folder before the lifecycle can become `drive_verified` or `drive_verified_fallback`.
