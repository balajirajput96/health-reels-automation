# REEL-0124 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Nasal Breathing & Nitric Oxide: Paranasal Sinus Vasodilation, 18% Higher Oxygen Saturation, Blood Pressure Regulation, and Air Filtration"—how breathing through the nose rather than the mouth delivers potent nitric oxide (NO) synthesized in the paranasal sinuses into the lungs, dilating pulmonary blood vessels and delivering up to 18% more oxygen to the brain and tissues while killing airborne microbes. It attributes findings to Nobel Laureate Dr. Louis Ignarro and James Nestor.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is mouth-breathers, athletes, snorers, and anyone with high blood pressure or chronic fatigue. The takeaway is that the mouth is for eating, the nose is for breathing—nasal breathing pressurizes and sterilizes air while flooding the bloodstream with vasodilating nitric oxide.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why breathing through your mouth instead of your nose steals nearly 20% of oxygen from your brain and organs.
2. The Paranasal Sinus Factory: our nasal sinuses producing continuous streams of Nitric Oxide (NO)—a Nobel Prize-winning signaling gas.
3. Nitric Oxide Vasodilation: NO traveling to the lower lobes of the lungs, relaxing blood vessels and expanding capillary uptake.
4. 18% Higher Oxygen Delivery: nasal resistance slowing airflow to allow optimal alveolar gas exchange and higher arterial oxygen saturation.
5. Triple Filtration & Sterilization: nasal cilia and mucus heating, humidifying, and trapping 98% of airborne viruses, allergens, and bacteria.
6. The Daytime & Nighttime Nasal Rule: keeping lips sealed throughout the day and using gentle mouth tape at night if waking with dry mouth.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute respiratory physiology to peer-reviewed literature (Ignarro, Nestor, Lundberg, Weitzberg). Use "Nasal Breathing," "Nitric Oxide (NO)," "Pulmonary Vasodilation," and "18% Higher Oxygenation."
