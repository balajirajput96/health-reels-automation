## Creative contract

**Audience:** Hindi-speaking general audience seeking evidence-grounded psychology education. **Format:** approximately 60 seconds, portrait 9:16 at 720×1280, Hindi narration and Devanagari captions. **Visual style:** restrained abstract research animation with a consistent neutral palette, simple questionnaire cards, domain tiles, and a person represented as a silhouette rather than a diagnosis or personality judgment. ## Evidence-safe thesis

Self-concept research does not measure a single, complete truth about a person. Depending on the instrument and population, a study may measure selected self-beliefs in domains such as academic, social, physical, emotional, behavioral, or general self-worth, often through self-report questionnaires. Self-concept clarity is a related structural facet—how clear, coherent, and stable self-beliefs feel—and should not be treated as the same thing as self-esteem, identity, diagnosis, or objective ability.
