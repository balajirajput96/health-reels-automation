# REEL-0160 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Decoy Effect (Asymmetric Dominance): Cognitive Choice Architecture, Relative Valuation, and The Economist Subscription Pricing Trap"—how adding an inferior third option (the decoy) shifts human preference overwhelmingly toward the most expensive target option by creating an illusion of extreme relative value. It attributes findings to Dr. Dan Ariely (MIT / Duke University) and Dr. Joel Huber.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is consumers, shoppers, marketers, and decision makers. The takeaway is that humans rarely choose things in absolute terms; companies manipulate your brain using decoys to make expensive options look like bargains. Look at whether you truly need the premium features before buying.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why adding a useless third popcorn size at the cinema tricks 80% of people into buying the largest, most expensive bucket.
2. Dan Ariely's Famous Economist Experiment: Web only ($59), Print only ($125), and Web + Print ($125).
3. The Decoy Mechanism: the 'Print only' option is an asymmetrically dominated decoy—nobody buys it, but it makes 'Web + Print' feel completely free.
4. The 84% Shift: without the decoy, 68% choose the cheap $59 option; with the decoy, 84% choose the expensive $125 option.
5. Relative vs Absolute Brain Evaluation: the human brain struggling to evaluate isolated monetary worth and defaulting to comparative contrasts.
6. The Anti-Decoy Defense: when choosing between products, remove the middle comparisons and evaluate each item strictly by what you will actually use.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute behavioral economics to peer-reviewed literature (Ariely, Huber, Payne, Puto). Use "The Decoy Effect," "Asymmetric Dominance," "Dan Ariely Experiment," and "Cognitive Pricing Architecture."

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
