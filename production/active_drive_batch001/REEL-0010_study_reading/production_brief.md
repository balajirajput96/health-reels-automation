# REEL-0010 — किसी habit study को सही तरह पढ़ना

## Unique angle

Reels 0002–0009 explain habit timelines, if–then plans, automaticity, self-monitoring, context limits, fresh starts, and contextual cues. Reel 0010 is a research-literacy reel: it teaches viewers to look past a behaviour-science headline and ask who was studied, what was manipulated or merely measured, what outcome was recorded, and what remains unknown.

## Format

Target: 9:16 vertical, approximately 60–80 seconds, Hindi (India), warm informative narration, burned-in captions, original scientific visuals, and a general-education disclaimer. No medical, mental-health, or assuranced behaviour-change claims.

## Six-scene plan

| Time | Visual action | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | A large research headline dissolves into four inspection windows, without readable text | Hook / research literacy |
| 10–22 s | Diverse participants and a study sample frame appear | Sample |
| 22–35 s | One panel shows a stable-context instruction; another shows natural app observations | Design distinction |
| 35–48 s | Gauges for automaticity and repetition-goal attainment appear as measured outcomes | Measurement |
| 48–62 s | A boundary line separates measured association from durable real-world change | Limit |
| 62–80 s | Four-question checklist becomes a calm, portable research-reading habit | Takeaway |

## Visual direction

Use the established deep navy, electric teal, and warm saffron scientific motion-graphics style. Keep a recurring young Indian adult researcher/learner and clean abstract study panels. Any study-like panels must be graphical only; do not generate readable text, numbers, fake labels, logos, or watermarks. Keep the lower quarter dark and caption-safe.

## Evidence boundaries

The 2022 context-stability paper used a manipulated university-student dataset and a separate measured app-user dataset. The paper examined context stability, automaticity, and habit-repetition goal attainment; it did not establish a universal cue formula or assurance durable health behaviour. The 2024 systematic review reports variability and evidence-quality limitations. The reel teaches how to interpret these boundaries rather than presenting a headline as a promise.
