# REEL-0011 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining that volunteering and structured community participation foster social connection and support mental well-being. It attributes findings to Mayo Clinic and Harvard Health public health research, emphasizing individual variation and avoiding diagnostic or clinical treatment claims.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking evidence-backed ways to build meaningful social connections and support wellbeing through community engagement. The takeaway is that contributing time to community work can help foster supportive relationships and reduce feelings of loneliness.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: an adult in a reflective moment, transitioning to an open community space.
2. Evidence: Mayo Clinic and Harvard Health study references highlighting community connection.
3. Mechanism: diverse community volunteers collaborating on a shared project.
4. Impact: supportive group interactions reducing feelings of isolation.
5. Meaning: a person experiencing a sense of purpose and contribution in group work.
6. Individual variation: recognition that individual experiences with community activities vary.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is for awareness and not medical advice.

## Visual constraints

Use a realistic, warm-toned editorial documentary style with soft natural light, inclusive adult representation, accessible community settings, lower-third caption-safe space, and no medical diagnosis framing or clinical promises.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, use readable multiline blocks, remain bottom-centered, and avoid health guarantees or clinical prescriptions.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute public health findings to Mayo Clinic Health System and Harvard Health. Use "may help" and "supportive." Do not claim that volunteering treats clinical depression, anxiety, or replaces professional health care.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
