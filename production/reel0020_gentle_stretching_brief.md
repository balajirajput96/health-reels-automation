# REEL-0020 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring how gentle stretching and varied movements support joint range of motion, muscle pliability, and daily physical ease. It attributes findings to physical medicine and exercise guidance from Harvard Health and Mayo Clinic.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking low-impact, accessible ways to maintain flexibility and movement variety in everyday life. The takeaway is that regular, gentle stretching within comfortable limits supports functional mobility.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: introducing the role of gentle stretching for daily mobility.
2. Evidence: Harvard Health and Mayo Clinic physical therapy insights.
3. Mechanism: stretching supporting joint range of motion and tissue flexibility.
4. Support: varied movement reducing stiffness and supporting physical ease.
5. Practice: introducing gentle movements into regular breaks.
6. Individual variation: moving within one's personal comfort range without straining.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is not physical therapy prescription.

## Visual constraints

Use calm, inclusive, gentle movement visuals (stretching arms, shoulders, gentle neck mobility), lower-third caption-safe area, and no extreme contortionism or orthopedic promises.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute stretching guidance to Harvard Health and Mayo Clinic. Use "may support" and "gentle practice." Do not claim stretching cures arthritis or structural orthopedic injuries.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
