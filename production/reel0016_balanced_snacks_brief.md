# REEL-0016 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining how combining multiple food groups (protein and fiber) in snacks supports sustained fullness (satiety) and steady digestion. It attributes findings to public health research from Harvard Health and peer-reviewed studies in PMC.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking practical, evidence-backed nutritional understanding to manage hunger between meals. The takeaway is that pairing protein and fiber sources promotes balanced digestion and sustained satiety.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: a question regarding between-meal hunger with a balanced snack flat lay.
2. Evidence: Harvard Health and PMC reference on protein and fiber synergy.
3. Mechanism: pairing protein and fiber slowing digestion rates.
4. Support: stable blood sugar response and sustained fullness feeling.
5. Individual variation: recognizing individual differences in nutritional needs.
6. Educational context: explaining this is general educational awareness.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is not medical or nutritional prescription.

## Visual constraints

Use a realistic, warm, clean editorial food aesthetic with natural lighting, inclusive representation, lower-third caption-safe area, and no extreme diet claims, weight-loss guarantees, or medical prescriptions.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute food pairing research to Harvard Health and PMC. Use "may help" and "supportive." Do not claim food combinations treat metabolic disorders or replace personalized healthcare guidance.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
