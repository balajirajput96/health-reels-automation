# REEL-0079 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "Interoception: The Body's Sixth Sense and How the Insular Cortex Reads Internal Signals"—how the human nervous system senses, interprets, and integrates internal physiological states (heartbeat, visceral motility, respiratory depth, thirst, muscle tension) through ascending vagal and spinothalamic pathways into the Anterior Insula, creating subjective emotional feelings and gut instincts (Antonio Damasio's Somatic Marker Hypothesis). It attributes findings to A.D. (Bud) Craig, Hugo Critchley, and interoceptive neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is people struggling with anxiety, panic, emotional disconnection, poor appetite regulation, or intuitive decision-making. The takeaway is that interoception (listening to internal bodily sensations) is the biological foundation of emotional intelligence and emotional regulation.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why you get a literal "gut feeling" or flutter in your heart before your conscious mind realizes you are in a risky situation.
2. What is Interoception: the nervous system's internal sensing network—the real "6th sense."
3. The Insular Cortex: how the insula maps heart rate, breathing, digestion, and core temperature into conscious emotion.
4. Heartbeat Detection Paradigm: laboratory tests measuring interoceptive accuracy (counting heartbeats without taking a pulse).
5. Antonio Damasio's Somatic Markers: bodily signals guiding fast, intuitive choices before logical rationalization kicks in.
6. Training Interoceptive Awareness: breath counting, body scans, and mindful somatic listening improving emotional regulation.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is interoceptive neuroscience and somatic psychology education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic Interoception neural pathways (Viscera/Heart -> Vagus/Spinothalamic -> Anterior Insula mapping -> Conscious Emotion / Intuition), lower-third caption-safe area, and no somatic symptom diagnosis.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute somatic neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Craig, Critchley, Damasio, Garfinkel). Use "interoception," "insular cortex," "somatic markers," and "visceral brain signaling." Do not diagnose panic disorder or hypochondria.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
