# REEL-0030 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining "Emotion Granularity"—the ability to construct precise emotional concepts (e.g., differentiating frustration from disappointment rather than just feeling "bad")—and how emotional precision supports flexible affective regulation. It attributes findings to affective neuroscience and cognitive psychology (Lisa Feldman Barrett et al.).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking evidence-backed tools to improve emotional self-awareness and regulation. The takeaway is that naming specific feelings provides the brain with targeted coping strategies.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why saying "I feel bad" is very different from identifying "disappointment" or "fatigue."
2. Evidence: affective neuroscience research on emotional granularity and constructivism.
3. Mechanism: high granularity giving the brain precise context to select effective coping behaviors.
4. Comparison: vague distress vs specific affective labels (e.g., irritation vs anxiety).
5. Practice: expanding emotional vocabulary through journaling and self-reflection.
6. Cognitive benefit: reduced rumination and improved adaptive emotional regulation.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is emotional science, not psychiatry.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, gradient emotional spectrum diagrams and precise vocabulary word maps, lower-third caption-safe area, and no psychiatric diagnostic claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute affective neuroscience to peer-reviewed emotion construction research. Use "emotional precision" and "adaptive regulation." Do not claim emotional vocabulary cures psychiatric mood disorders.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
