# REEL-0032 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining "Affect Labeling in Context"—how putting words to emotional states during acute, high-arousal situations engages the right ventrolateral prefrontal cortex (rvlPFC) and down-regulates amygdala reactivity.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking a neurobiological understanding of why verbalizing distress reduces physiological arousal. The takeaway is that linguistic labeling provides top-down neural dampening of emotional reactivity.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why speaking out what you feel immediately lowers internal tension.
2. Evidence: neuroimaging (fMRI) studies on affect labeling and amygdala modulation.
3. Mechanism: prefrontal cortex (rvlPFC) activation during linguistic categorization of emotion.
4. Physiological brake: reduced autonomic arousal and stabilized skin conductance.
5. Practice: using simple, descriptive phrases during tense moments ("I feel anxious right now").
6. Nuance: noting that labeling supports regulation rather than instantly erasing difficult circumstances.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is affective neuroscience education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, scientific brain network schematics (rvlPFC to amygdala connectivity arrows), lower-third caption-safe area, and no psychiatric cure claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute affective neuroscience to peer-reviewed fMRI affect labeling research (Lieberman et al.). Use "may modulate" and "down-regulate." Do not claim affect labeling cures panic disorder or clinical PTSD.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
