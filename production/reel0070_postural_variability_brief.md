# REEL-0070 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel reframing desk ergonomics around comfortable postural variation rather than a single perfect posture. The reel distinguishes a small workplace intervention from an exploratory group comparison and avoids medical treatment claims.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults who spend time at a desk. The takeaway is modest: if comfortable, periodically vary position and add gentle movement instead of forcing one fixed pose. This is not a diagnosis, treatment, or guarantee against pain.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals animated with deterministic motion if official visual quotas permit. Native video generation is optional and may be used only if its official quota is available. If image generation remains exhausted, do not use unrelated visuals; record a blocked preproduction state.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: an adult at a neutral desk shifts from one comfortable seated position to another without dramatic posture correction.
2. Context: a realistic office workstation with ordinary low-movement desk work, no medical or diagnostic framing.
3. Intervention: a small call-center-style workspace where a subtle reminder prompts a worker to stand or adjust position; do not display generated software text or fake study screenshots.
4. Evidence boundary: a research notebook and neutral study imagery, visually signaling “small study” and “short-term” without adding text.
5. Exploratory comparison: two neutral laptop-work scenes with different people, no labels implying diagnosis or causation.
6. Practical cue: an adult adjusts the chair, moves shoulders gently, and changes position while keeping the work area safe and uncluttered.
7. Safety: the person pauses and seeks qualified help when persistent pain or concerning symptoms are suggested, without showing distress or injury.

## Visual constraints

Use a consistent realistic editorial documentary style with natural light, inclusive adult representation, neutral workwear, uncluttered backgrounds, lower-third caption-safe space, and no logos, brand labels, fake research graphics, diagnostic imagery, or text-bearing AI graphics. Avoid exaggerated “before/after” posture transformations.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, use readable multiline blocks, remain bottom-centered, and avoid covering faces, hands, or workstation details. Do not add posture angles, timers, stretches, or medical claims that are absent from the approved script.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute the short-term discomfort finding to the small call-center intervention. Describe the Nature paper as an exploratory, non-randomized group comparison. Do not claim that posture changes cure pain, prevent musculoskeletal disorders, treat headache, or improve productivity. Avoid a universal “correct posture” rule and avoid advising viewers to exercise through pain.

## Disclosure

If produced, state that visuals are AI-generated reference images animated with deterministic motion. Record any official quota limitation. The package remains unpublished until separate editorial review.
