# REEL-0102 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Sleep Pressure & Adenosine: Why We Get Tired, Adenosine Accumulation, and How Caffeine Blocks Sleep Signals"—how cellular ATP metabolism creates adenosine as a byproduct during wakefulness, binding to brain receptors to build "sleep pressure", and why caffeine creates an afternoon crash by temporarily masking adenosine receptors. It attributes findings to Matthew Walker, Clifford Saper, and sleep researchers.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults experiencing fatigue, sleep problems, or relying heavily on coffee to get through the day. The takeaway is that sleep pressure is a chemical clock driven by adenosine—caffeine doesn't destroy tiredness, it merely silences the alarm until it wears off.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why you feel progressively heavier as the day goes on and why coffee only pauses tiredness rather than deleting it.
2. The Chemical Hourglass: ATP energy consumption in neurons producing adenosine throughout the waking day.
3. Adenosine Receptors & Sleep Pressure: adenosine binding to A1/A2A receptors in the basal forebrain to slow neural firing and trigger sleepiness.
4. How Caffeine Works: caffeine having a similar molecular structure, slotting into adenosine receptors and blocking the tiredness signal.
5. The Caffeine Crash Phenomenon: while caffeine blocks receptors, adenosine continues to build up in the background, causing a flood when caffeine metabolizes.
6. The 90-Minute Rule: delaying morning caffeine by 90 minutes and clearing adenosine with 7-9 hours of restorative sleep.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute adenosine research to peer-reviewed literature (Walker, Saper, Fredholm, Basheer). Use "Sleep Pressure," "Adenosine molecule," "A1/A2A receptors," and "Caffeine receptor blockade." Do not diagnose clinical insomnia.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
