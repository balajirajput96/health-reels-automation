# REEL-0101 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Neuroscience of Gratitude: Medial Prefrontal Cortex (mPFC) Activation, Dopamine & Serotonin Modulation, and Amygdala Calming"—how practicing deliberate gratitude structurally shifts neural pathways, enhances mood-regulating neurotransmitters, and down-regulates fear/stress reactivity. It attributes findings to Robert Emmons, Glenn Fox, and neuroimaging studies.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking practical, science-backed mental wellness techniques. The takeaway is that gratitude is not toxic positivity or wishful thinking—it is an active neural exercise that trains the medial prefrontal cortex to release serotonin and dopamine while dampening baseline stress circuits.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why writing down 3 things you are grateful for shifts brain chemistry faster than positive thinking alone.
2. The Neuroimaging Discovery: fMRI scans showing gratitude lighting up the medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC) and anterior cingulate.
3. Neurotransmitter Surge: gratitude triggering simultaneous dopamine (motivation/reward) and serotonin (contentment/wellbeing) release.
4. Down-regulating the Amygdala: how active appreciation sends inhibitory signals to the amygdala, reducing anxiety and physiological stress.
5. The Gratitude Muscle: neuroplasticity strengthening positive appraisal pathways over time, reducing default negativity bias.
6. The 2-Minute Protocol: receiving genuine gratitude or recalling a time someone helped you for maximum neural activation.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute gratitude neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Emmons, Fox, Damasio, Keltner). Use "Neuroscience of Gratitude," "medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC)," "dopamine and serotonin," and "amygdala modulation." Do not claim gratitude cures clinical major depressive disorder.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
