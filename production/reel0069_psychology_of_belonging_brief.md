# REEL-0069 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring "The Psychology and Neurobiology of Belonging: Social Exclusion as Physical Pain"—how the fundamental human drive for social belonging (Baumeister & Leary's Belongingness Hypothesis) is hardwired into the nervous system, such that social rejection and ostracism recruit the same dorsal anterior cingulate cortex (dACC) and anterior insula circuits that register physical pain. It attributes findings to Roy Baumeister, Naomi Eisenberger, and Matthew Lieberman.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults experiencing isolation, workplace exclusion, loneliness, or community disconnection. The takeaway is that humans are obligate social primates whose brains register social exclusion as a literal physical threat to survival; building authentic small social bonds protects cognitive and physical longevity.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why being excluded from a group or ignored in a conversation hurts physically inside your chest.
2. The Belonging Hypothesis: Baumeister & Leary's foundational theory—the need to belong as a primary human drive like food or sleep.
3. The Cyberball Experiment: Naomi Eisenberger's fMRI study showing social exclusion activating the physical pain matrix (dACC).
4. Evolutionary Logic: in ancestral environments, social isolation from the tribe meant guaranteed physical mortality.
5. Biological Costs of Rejection: chronic loneliness triggering systemic inflammatory cascades (IL-6) and sleep fragmentation.
6. The Remedy — Deep Dyadic Bonds: you don't need hundreds of followers; just 2-3 trusted, authentic relationships satisfy the evolutionary need.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is social neuroscience and health psychology education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic social pain matrix diagrams (Social Rejection -> dACC neural flare = Physical Pain Circuit overlap), lower-third caption-safe area, and no clinical depression promises.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute social psychology to peer-reviewed belonging literature (Baumeister & Leary, Eisenberger, Lieberman, Cacioppo). Use "need to belong," "social exclusion," "dACC social pain," and "dyadic connection." Do not diagnose clinical social anxiety.

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
