# REEL-0151 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR) & Yoga Nidra: Striatal Dopamine Restoration, Cortisol Reduction, and Neuroplasticity Consolidation"—how 20 minutes of NSDR/Yoga Nidra shifts the autonomic nervous system into theta brainwave states, accelerating physical and mental recovery, blunting systemic cortisol, and restoring striatal dopamine reserves by up to 65%. It attributes findings to Dr. Andrew Huberman (Stanford) and Dr. Troels Kjaer (Kennedy Institute Copenhagen).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone experiencing midday mental burnout, poor sleep debt, or high chronic stress. The takeaway is that a 20-minute NSDR session restores working memory, executive function, and dopamine reserves faster than a caffeine hit or fragmented nap.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why 20 minutes of guided Non-Sleep Deep Rest restores your brain dopamine by 65% without needing to actually fall asleep.
2. The Dopamine Depletion Dilemma: prolonged cognitive focus and high stress depleting ventral striatal dopamine reserves, causing brain fog.
3. The Copenhagen PET Scan Discovery: Dr. Troels Kjaer showing a 65% surge in striatal dopamine during Yoga Nidra / NSDR.
4. Theta Brainwave State: conscious relaxation inducing 4-8 Hz theta waves, calming the amygdala and blunting cortisol.
5. Synaptic Neuroplasticity Consolidation: accelerated memory retention and skill learning when followed by a 20-minute NSDR session.
6. The 20-Minute NSDR Daily Protocol: lie down flat in the afternoon, put on headphones with guided NSDR audio, relax your eyes and body without forcing sleep.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute autonomic neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Huberman, Kjaer, Lou, Parker). Use "Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR)," "Yoga Nidra," "65% Striatal Dopamine," and "Theta Brainwaves."
