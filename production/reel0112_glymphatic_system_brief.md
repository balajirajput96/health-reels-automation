# REEL-0112 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Glymphatic System: Brain's Nightly Dishwasher, Beta-Amyloid Toxin Flush, Deep Sleep, and Lateral Sleep Posture"—how during non-REM slow-wave sleep, glial cells shrink by 60%, allowing cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) to rush through interstitial spaces and wash away toxic metabolic waste (amyloid-beta, tau proteins). It attributes findings to Maiken Nedergaard and sleep neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone interested in cognitive longevity, preventing neurodegeneration, and understanding why sleep is biologically non-negotiable. The takeaway is that sleep is not passive downtime—it is an active neurological plumbing cycle; side sleeping (lateral position) facilitates the most efficient glymphatic clearance.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why sleeping less than 6 hours leaves your brain physically soaked in metabolic toxins the next morning.
2. Discovery of the Glymphatic System: Maiken Nedergaard discovering the brain's specialized waste clearance network operating primarily during sleep.
3. Glial Cell Shrinkage: glial cells shrinking by up to 60% during deep slow-wave sleep, widening channels for fluid flow.
4. Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF) Flush: CSF pulsing through brain tissue, washing away beta-amyloid plaques and tau proteins linked to Alzheimer's.
5. Lateral Sleep Position Advantage: studies showing side sleeping optimizes gravitational CSF flow and arterial pulsatility for waste removal.
6. The 7-8 Hour Deep Sleep Rule: avoiding late-night alcohol/heavy meals to protect stage 3 slow-wave sleep.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute glymphatic neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Nedergaard, Xie, Lee, Walker). Use "Glymphatic System," "Cerebrospinal Fluid," "Amyloid-Beta Clearance," and "Slow-Wave Deep Sleep."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
