# REEL-0033 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining "Cognitive Reappraisal"—an emotion regulation strategy where an individual reinterprets the meaning of a situation or physiological arousal (e.g., viewing racing heart as excitement/readiness rather than fear/failure) to alter its emotional impact. It attributes findings to cognitive neuroscience and psychophysiology (Gross et al., Jamieson et al.).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults facing performance pressure or stressful events. The takeaway is that changing cognitive appraisal modulates physiological distress and performance outcomes.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: racing heartbeat before a presentation—is it fear or readiness?
2. Evidence: psychophysiology and cognitive reappraisal studies (threat vs challenge appraisals).
3. Mechanism: prefrontal cognitive restructuring altering emotional trajectory before full response occurs.
4. Cardiovascular difference: challenge state improving blood flow vs threat state causing constriction.
5. Practice: consciously reframing somatic sensations as resources ("My body is preparing me to perform").
6. Boundary: distinguishing healthy reappraisal from toxic positivity or emotional suppression.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is emotion regulation science.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic dual-pathway diagrams comparing threat vs challenge appraisals, lower-third caption-safe area, and no medical cure claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive science to peer-reviewed reappraisal and stress mindset research. Use "may reframe" and "cognitive restructuring." Do not claim reappraisal replaces clinical anxiety therapy.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
