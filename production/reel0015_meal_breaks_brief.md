# REEL-0015 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining that taking consistent, scheduled meal breaks during busy workdays supports cognitive performance, reduces fatigue, and helps maintain energy levels. It attributes findings to public health research from Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and PMC literature on workplace wellness.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is working adults seeking evidence-backed ways to manage busy workdays without neglecting nutrition and rest. The takeaway is that scheduling short, dedicated breaks for meals supports focus and sustainable daily well-being.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: a busy desk setting where a person pauses to step away for a scheduled break.
2. Evidence: Harvard T.H. Chan Public Health reference on stress management and nutrition.
3. Mechanism: continuous un-interrupted work leading to fatigue and decision stress.
4. Support: PMC research on scheduled breaks and mindful eating.
5. Practice: stepping away from screens for 15-20 minutes to eat and recharge.
6. Individual variation: recognizing that work demands vary and break times can be adapted flexibly.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is for awareness and not medical advice.

## Visual constraints

Use a realistic, soft-lit workplace documentary style with inclusive adult representation, clean office/breakroom settings, lower-third caption-safe area, and no extreme diet claims, weight-loss advice, or clinical prescriptions.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute workplace nutrition and break research to Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and PMC. Use "may help" and "supportive." Do not claim meal breaks treat metabolic or medical conditions or replace nutritional consulting.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
