# REEL-0086 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Habit Formation: Basal Ganglia, Action Chunking, and the Cue-Routine-Reward Cycle"—how repeated actions shift control from the prefrontal cortex to the dorsolateral striatum of the basal ganglia through a process called "action chunking", making healthy habits automatic and effortless. It attributes findings to Ann Graybiel, Charles Duhigg, and habit neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is individuals trying to build sustainable healthy habits or break unwanted routines. The takeaway is that habits are built by anchoring a routine to an existing cue and delivering an immediate sensory or emotional reward—allowing the basal ganglia to chunk the behavior so it requires zero prefrontal willpower.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why starting a new habit feels exhausting at first, but after 60 days happens completely automatically.
2. The Brain Shift: Prefrontal Cortex (high effort) shifting execution to the Basal Ganglia / Dorsolateral Striatum (automatic chunking).
3. Action Chunking: Ann Graybiel's discovery that the brain groups complex sequences into a single neural macro-command.
4. The Habit Loop: Cue (Trigger) -> Routine (Behavior) -> Reward (Dopamine Spike).
5. Cue Anchoring: linking new habits to existing daily triggers (e.g., after brushing teeth -> 5 deep breaths).
6. Friction Reduction: reducing activation energy for positive routines while adding friction to unwanted habits.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute habit neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Graybiel, Duhigg, Wood, Lally). Use "basal ganglia," "action chunking," "dorsolateral striatum," and "cue-routine-reward loop." Do not claim addiction or OCD can be solved by simple habit loops.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
