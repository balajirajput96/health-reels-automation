# REEL-0072 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "The Social Psychology and Neuroscience of Dehumanization: Stereotypes, Outgroup Biases, and mPFC Inactivity"—how the human brain categorizes outgroup individuals along dimensions of Warmth and Competence (Susan Fiske's Stereotype Content Model), and how severe outgroup prejudice or disgust can fail to engage the medial Prefrontal Cortex (mPFC), treating human beings cognitively as objects or numbers. It attributes findings to Susan T. Fiske, Lasana Harris, and Nick Haslam.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is citizens, leaders, communicators, and general adults. The takeaway is that tribal prejudice and outgroup hostility actively suppress neural empathy; consciously humanizing others by recognizing shared emotional vulnerabilities and individual narratives re-engages the brain's social perception circuitry.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why humans find it easy to feel deep empathy for friends and family, but can feel surprisingly indifferent toward distant strangers.
2. The Stereotype Content Model: Susan Fiske's matrix mapping social groups on two evolutionary axes: Warmth (friend or foe) and Competence (capability).
3. The Neural Gap: fMRI research by Lasana Harris showing low-warmth/low-competence outgroups fail to activate the social brain (mPFC).
4. Animalistic vs Mechanistic Dehumanization: Nick Haslam's framework—denying people culture and civility versus denying them warmth and emotion.
5. Ingroup Favoritism Danger: how tribal "us versus them" framing turns ethical humans into uncaring observers.
6. The Humanizing Antidote: asking about an individual's personal story, favorite food, or hopes instantly restores mPFC social resonance.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is social psychology and cognitive neuroscience education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic Stereotype Content 2x2 grids (Warmth vs Competence -> Ingroup Empathy vs Outgroup Suppression), lower-third caption-safe area, and no political activism.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute social neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Fiske, Harris, Haslam, Cikara). Use "Stereotype Content Model," "mPFC social perception," "dehumanization," and "humanizing perspective." Do not make political or defamatory attacks.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
