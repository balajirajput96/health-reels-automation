# REEL-0121 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Morning Sunlight & Circadian Anchoring: Melanopsin ipRGCs, Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN), Cortisol Awakening Response, and the 14-Hour Melatonin Timer"—how viewing 10-15 minutes of early morning natural sunlight resets the master circadian clock, fires a healthy morning cortisol surge for daytime alertness, and starts the timer for nightly melatonin release. It attributes findings to Dr. Satchin Panda, Dr. Clifford Saper, and Dr. Andrew Huberman.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone struggling with morning grogginess, insomnia, low energy, or irregular sleep schedules. The takeaway is that light is the primary biological synchronizer (zeitgeber); getting natural photons into your eyes within 60 minutes of waking anchors your energy and sleep architecture.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why looking at the morning sun for 10 minutes cures insomnia better than sleeping pills.
2. The Retinal Sensors (ipRGCs): specialized intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells sensing blue-yellow sunlight wavelengths.
3. The Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN): direct neural wiring from eyes to the brain's central clock in the hypothalamus.
4. The Cortisol Awakening Surge: sunlight firing a healthy morning cortisol spike that eliminates brain fog and elevates daytime mood.
5. The 14-Hour Melatonin Clock: morning light exposure setting a biochemical countdown timer for melatonin release 14-16 hours later.
6. The Window Glass Mistake: why viewing light through glass blocks critical wavelengths and takes 50x longer; go outdoors directly.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute circadian neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Panda, Saper, Huberman, Berson). Use "Morning Sunlight," "Melanopsin ipRGCs," "Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN)," and "Circadian Anchoring."

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
