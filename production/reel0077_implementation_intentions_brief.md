# REEL-0077 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining implementation intentions as a flexible “if–then” planning strategy that links a specific situation with a feasible action. The reel separates average research findings from personal guarantees and emphasizes adapting plans to real constraints.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults who set goals but struggle to start. The takeaway is to convert a vague goal into one specific cue and one small, safe response, then revise the plan if it is unrealistic.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals animated with deterministic motion if official visual quotas permit. Native video generation is optional and may be used only if its official quota is available. If image generation remains exhausted, do not use unrelated visuals; record a blocked preproduction state.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: an adult looks at a vague goal note, then pauses before an ordinary routine transition; no productivity pressure.
2. Definition: simple visual bridge between a cue icon and an action icon, with no generated text; captions explain the words.
3. Why it may help: a person notices a routine cue and begins a small action, avoiding “automatic brain” claims.
4. Example: dinner ends, shoes are already near a doorway, and the person considers a short walk; no fixed duration or health claim.
5. Evidence boundary: neutral research pages and varied everyday outcomes, with no fake meta-analysis graphics or success percentages.
6. Make it realistic: the person revises the plan when weather, pain, access, schedule, or safety changes; no blame imagery.
7. Safety: inclusive representation of different abilities and circumstances, showing personal adaptation rather than a universal prescription.

## Visual constraints

Use a consistent realistic editorial documentary style with soft natural light, inclusive adult representation, varied access needs, uncluttered environments, lower-third caption-safe space, and no logos, fake research screenshots, brain-rewiring visuals, productivity dashboards, medical exercise prescriptions, or readable AI-generated text. The cue and action should look feasible and ordinary.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, use readable multiline blocks, remain bottom-centered, and avoid adding fixed schedules, habit guarantees, health outcomes, or “discipline” judgments absent from the script.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute the framework and average evidence to the NCI reference and Wieber et al. review. Use “may help” and “average results.” Do not claim that implementation intentions rewire the brain, create automatic habits for everyone, improve weight or health markers, or replace personalized professional guidance.

## Disclosure

If produced, state that visuals are AI-generated reference images animated with deterministic motion. Record any official quota limitation. The package remains unpublished until separate editorial review.
