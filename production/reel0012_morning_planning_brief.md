# REEL-0012 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining that starting the morning with structured planning and realistic priorities can help reduce decision fatigue and support daily mental well-being. It attributes findings to public health insights from Harvard Health and the American Psychological Association.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking evidence-backed ways to manage daily stress and set manageable priorities. The takeaway is that focusing on small, realistic morning milestones rather than overwhelming task lists supports sustainable routines.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: a calm morning scene with a person reviewing a simple daily outline by natural window light.
2. Evidence: Harvard Health research reference on structured goal-setting.
3. Mechanism: replacing decision fatigue with small, structured priorities.
4. Support: APA public health insights on incremental habit formation.
5. Focus: transitioning from overwhelming task lists to 2-3 manageable milestones.
6. Individual variation: adjusting morning routines according to individual pace and life demands.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is for general awareness and not medical advice.

## Visual constraints

Use a realistic, soft-lit editorial documentary style with clean accessible spaces, inclusive adult representation, lower-third caption-safe areas, and no commercial productivity graphics or medical claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid prescriptions.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute insights to Harvard Health and the American Psychological Association. Use "may help" and "supportive." Do not claim morning planning treats clinical anxiety or guarantees productivity outcomes.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
