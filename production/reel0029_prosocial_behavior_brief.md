# REEL-0029 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring the psychological science of "prosocial behavior"—how helping others, volunteering, and small acts of kindness trigger positive affective feedback ("warm glow") and support subjective well-being. It attributes findings to social psychology and neurobiology.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults interested in the science of happiness and interpersonal relationships. The takeaway is that prosocial actions support both the recipient and the giver's emotional well-being.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why helping someone else often leaves us feeling genuinely happier.
2. Evidence: social psychology and neuroimaging research on prosocial giving and positive affect.
3. Mechanism: activation of reward circuitry and release of prosocial neurochemicals (oxytocin, dopamine).
4. Term: the "warm glow" effect and its reinforcement of social bonds.
5. Practice: introducing spontaneous small acts of assistance in everyday life.
6. Boundaries: balancing prosocial giving with personal self-care to avoid burnout.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is social science education.

## Visual constraints

Use warm, uplifting, minimalist community and interaction visuals, lower-third caption-safe area, and no medical cure claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute social psychology to peer-reviewed prosocial spending and altruism research. Use "may support" and "positive affect." Do not claim prosocial acts cure clinical mood disorders.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
