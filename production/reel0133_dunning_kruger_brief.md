# REEL-0133 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Dunning-Kruger Effect: Metacognitive Blindspots, 'Mount Stupid', Cognitive Humility, and Why Beginners Feel Like Experts"—how individuals with low competence in a domain substantially overestimate their knowledge because the very skill required to evaluate competence is absent; whereas real experts tend to underestimate their relative ability. It attributes findings to Dr. David Dunning and Dr. Justin Kruger.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is learners, decision-makers, and curious minds. The takeaway is that a little knowledge creates dangerous overconfidence; true mastery starts when you realize how much you don't know (cognitive humility).

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why reading a single article or watching a 5-minute video makes people feel like they know more than professors.
2. The 1999 Dunning-Kruger Discovery: David Dunning and Justin Kruger mapping the cognitive curve of confidence versus real competence.
3. The Peak of "Mount Stupid": beginners acquiring minimal introductory facts and experiencing a burst of illusory superiority.
4. Metacognitive Deficit: lacking the domain depth needed to even recognize what mistakes you are making.
5. The "Valley of Despair": as you study deeper, you realize the massive ocean of unknowns and your confidence drops to healthy humility.
6. The True Mastery Curve: experts possessing high competence but knowing that knowledge is vast and nuanced.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive bias science to peer-reviewed literature (Dunning, Kruger, Ehrlinger, Schlösser). Use "Dunning-Kruger Effect," "Metacognitive Deficit," "Mount Stupid," and "Cognitive Humility."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
