# REEL-0135 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Adult Neurogenesis & BDNF: Hippocampal Stem Cell Proliferation, Miracle-Gro for the Brain, Aerobic Exercise, and Intermittent Fasting"—debunking the outdated myth that adults cannot grow new brain cells. The dentate gyrus of the hippocampus produces around 700 new neurons every single day, accelerated by Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) stimulated by running, deep sleep, and cognitive challenges. It attributes findings to Dr. Fred Gage and Dr. Gerd Kempermann.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone worried about mental decline, memory loss, or cognitive burnout. The takeaway is that your brain is neuroplastic throughout life; consistent aerobic movement and fasting trigger BDNF to birth, mature, and integrate new neurons into your memory banks.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: debunking the old medical belief that you are born with all the brain cells you'll ever have.
2. The Dentate Gyrus Discovery: Salk Institute neuroscientists proving that the adult human hippocampus creates ~700 new brain cells daily.
3. BDNF "Miracle-Gro": Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor acting as neural fertilizer that protects, feeds, and matures nascent neurons.
4. Aerobic Cardio as Driver: running or vigorous cycling releasing lactate and irisin that trigger a massive surge in hippocampal BDNF.
5. Fasting & Autophagy: caloric restriction and 14-16h intermittent fasting stimulating stem cell renewal and synaptic plasticity.
6. The Learning Integration: new brain cells only survive if challenged with new skills, languages, or complex problem-solving.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute adult neurogenesis to peer-reviewed literature (Gage, Kempermann, van Praag, Mattson). Use "Adult Neurogenesis," "Hippocampal Dentate Gyrus," "BDNF Brain Fertilizer," and "Synaptic Plasticity."

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
