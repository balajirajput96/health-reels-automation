## Creative direction

The reel should feel like a calm, evidence-led science explainer rather than a diet challenge. The hook connects daily energy with timing without promising a result. Visual language should show a 24-hour clock, morning-to-evening light, a real meal plate, and abstract peripheral-clock/circadian graphics. Do not use weight-loss before/after imagery, a fasting timer, a “perfect meal time” graphic, or a medical-looking glucose claim that is stronger than the source evidence.
