# REEL-0094 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Psychological Safety: Amygdala Threat Reduction, Google Aristotle Project, and High-Performing Team Brains"—how psychological safety (the belief that one will not be punished or humiliated for speaking up, asking questions, or making mistakes) calms amygdala hypervigilance, unlocking prefrontal executive function, creative risk-taking, and collective intelligence. It attributes findings to Amy Edmondson, Google Aristotle Project, and organizational neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is managers, team leaders, students, and employees in high-stress work environments. The takeaway is that fear and punishment cripple team performance by triggering threat responses in the brain; psychological safety is the single strongest predictor of team innovation and high performance.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why the smartest teams with top talent often fail, while average teams achieve extraordinary innovation.
2. Amy Edmondson's Discovery: Amy Edmondson's research at Harvard showing that high-performing hospital teams actually report more mistakes because they feel safe to share them.
3. The Google Project Aristotle Finding: Google's 2-year study analyzing 180 teams finding psychological safety to be the #1 factor in team success.
4. The Brain Under Fear: fear and public humiliation activating the amygdala fight-or-flight state, hijacking prefrontal executive capacity.
5. Unlocking Prefrontal Innovation: safety allowing the prefrontal cortex to take intellectual risks, ask tough questions, and share unconventional ideas.
6. Building Safety in Daily Work: framing work as learning problems, acknowledging manager fallibility, and responding with curiosity to errors.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute psychological safety to peer-reviewed literature (Edmondson, Duhigg/Google Aristotle Project, West). Use "Psychological Safety," "Amygdala Threat Response," "Prefrontal Executive Capacity," and "Project Aristotle." Do not offer business consulting services.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
