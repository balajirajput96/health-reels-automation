# REEL-0130 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Neuroscience of Procrastination: Limbic System Amygdala Hijack vs Prefrontal Cortex Executive Control, and Why It Is an Emotional Regulation Failure Rather Than Laziness"—how procrastination is not poor time management or character defect, but a subconscious survival coping mechanism where the emotional limbic system avoids negative feelings (boredom, fear of failure, self-doubt) linked to a task. It attributes findings to Dr. Timothy Pychyl and Dr. Fuschia Sirois.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is chronic procrastinators, perfectionists, students, and professionals feeling immense guilt over task delay. The takeaway is that you cannot fix procrastination with planners; you fix it by addressing the immediate negative emotion through lowering friction to the first 2 minutes.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why you want to finish an important project, yet find yourself cleaning your room or scrolling social media for 2 hours instead.
2. The Neural Battleground: the evolutionary conflict between the impulsive, ancient Limbic System and the logical Prefrontal Cortex.
3. Emotional Regulation Failure: Timothy Pychyl showing procrastination is an immediate mood-repair reaction to negative emotions (anxiety, dread, perfectionism).
4. Short-Term Dopamine Relief: dodging the task giving an instant opioid/dopamine hit of relief, reinforcing the bad habit loop.
5. The Future Self Gap: fMRI scans showing the brain thinking of your "future self" as a complete stranger, dumping work on them.
6. The 2-Minute Micro-Action: reduce the entry barrier so low (e.g. open the doc and write 1 word) that the amygdala does not trigger fear.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute procrastination neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Pychyl, Sirois, Steel, Hershfield). Use "Procrastination Neuroscience," "Limbic Hijack," "Emotional Regulation," and "2-Minute Micro-Action."
