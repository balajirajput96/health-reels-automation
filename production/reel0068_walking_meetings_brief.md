# REEL-0068 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel for sedentary office workers explaining that some small meetings may be converted into short walking meetings, while clearly separating feasibility evidence from unsupported productivity or health promises.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults with desk-based work who want low-barrier ways to add movement without treating one behavior as a medical prescription. The takeaway is optional and conditional: when a small group agrees and the meeting does not require screens, documents, confidential information, or unsafe movement, a short agenda-led walk may be considered.

## Format

The output is a portrait 9:16 video of approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific AI-generated reference visuals animated with deterministic motion. No native multi-clip video generation is required for this concept. If image-generation quota limits the number of references, the final metadata must disclose the exact fallback and must not use unrelated stock-like substitutes.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: a small office group preparing to leave a seated meeting area, with a clear route and no visible brand marks.
2. Pilot context: a symbolic university-office corridor with two or three colleagues walking side by side, no claim of a real study reenactment.
3. Evidence boundary: a neutral split visual contrasting a modest research note with a crossed-out guarantee symbol, without generated text or numerical overlays.
4. General movement: an adult walking outdoors while a calendar-like sequence of small movement moments is suggested abstractly, without exact schedule prescriptions.
5. Fit check: a small group walking with an agenda card kept closed or held safely, no laptops or confidential paperwork visible.
6. Practical setup: one person leading, another checking time, and a simple note-taking action after returning to a desk.
7. Safety boundary: accessible, well-lit path and a calm decision to remain seated when weather, pain, balance, privacy, or accessibility conditions are unsuitable.

## Visual constraints

Use a consistent realistic editorial documentary style with natural daylight, clean neutral workplace and outdoor settings, inclusive adult representation, generous lower-third caption-safe space, and no logos, brand labels, fabricated study screenshots, diagnostic imagery, or text-bearing AI graphics. Walking should be shown as ordinary comfortable movement rather than intense exercise.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, use controlled line breaks, remain bottom-centered and readable, and avoid covering faces or the walking path. Mixed Hindi-English workplace terms may remain where they are already in the approved script.

## Evidence and safety gates

Do not claim that walking meetings improve productivity, creativity, mood, weight, disease risk, or health for everyone. Attribute the feasibility and modest activity observation to the small CDC pilot. Attribute the smaller-chunk principle to CDC general activity guidance. Keep the acute biomarker meta-analysis separate from walking-meeting outcomes. Include accessibility, pain, balance, heat, weather, privacy, traffic, and workplace-policy considerations.

## Disclosure

The final package must state that visuals are AI-generated reference images animated with deterministic motion. Any official generation quota limit must be recorded. This package is general education only, is not individualized medical or exercise advice, and is not publication-ready until editorial review is complete.
