# REEL-0096 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Epigenetics: Lifestyle, DNA Methylation, Histone Modification, and How Your Choices Turn Genes On or Off"—how lifestyle factors (exercise, sleep, nutrition, chronic stress) act as chemical switches (via DNA methylation and histone acetylation) that alter gene expression without changing the underlying genetic sequence. It attributes findings to Michael Meaney, Moshe Szyf, and epigenetic neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults interested in longevity, preventive health, and behavioral biology. The takeaway is that DNA is not a fixed blueprint dictating your destiny—your daily habits actively control chemical tags that silence harmful genes and activate protective ones.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why your DNA is not a fixed fate written in stone, but a dynamic instrument played by your daily choices.
2. What is Epigenetics: epi (above) genetics—chemical tags attached to DNA that dictate which genes are read or silenced.
3. DNA Methylation & Histones: methyl groups locking genes off; histone acetylation unspooling DNA so genes can be read.
4. The Lifestyle Signal: exercise, deep sleep, and nutrient-rich food sending signals that turn on BDNF and antioxidant production.
5. Stress & Inflammation Tags: chronic stress adding methyl tags that down-regulate stress resilience receptors in the brain.
6. Reversibility: positive lifestyle changes removing harmful epigenetic tags and restoring optimal gene expression over time.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute epigenetics research to peer-reviewed literature (Meaney, Szyf, Jirtle, Nestler). Use "Epigenetics," "DNA methylation," "histone acetylation," and "gene expression." Do not claim epigenetics cures genetic diseases or cancer.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
