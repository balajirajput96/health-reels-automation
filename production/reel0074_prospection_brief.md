# REEL-0074 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring "Episodic Future Thinking (Prospection): How the Brain Simulates Tomorrow to Make Better Choices Today"—how the human brain uses the Default Mode Network (hippocampus, medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate) to vividly project past episodic memories into hypothetical future scenarios (prospection), and how vivid future simulation dramatically reduces impulsive temporal discounting and strengthens long-term self-control. It attributes findings to Daniel Gilbert, Karl Szpunar, and cognitive neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is people struggling with procrastination, impulsive spending, poor health habits, or future planning. The takeaway is that humans are "prospective animals" (Homo Prospectus); vividly visualizing your specific future self navigating a situation curbs short-term impulsive cravings.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why humans are the only animals that spend hours imagining scenarios that haven't happened yet—like next month's vacation or next year's goals.
2. The Concept: Prospection & Episodic Future Thinking—the brain as a mental time machine.
3. Default Mode Network: how the brain recycles past memory bricks (hippocampus) to construct vivid future architectural simulations.
4. Reducing Temporal Discounting: fMRI research showing vivid future self-visualization reduces preference for immediate impulsive rewards.
5. Empathy for Your Future Self: treating "Future You" as a real best friend rather than an abstract stranger.
6. Mental Contrasting Technique: pairing a vivid future wish with the realistic obstacles you will face today (Gabriele Oettingen's WOOP framework).
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is cognitive neuroscience and decision science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic mental simulation timelines (Past Episodic Retrieval -> Hippocampal Assembly -> Vivid Future Prospection -> Immediate Impulse Reduction), lower-third caption-safe area, and no financial investment guarantees.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive science to peer-reviewed literature (Gilbert, Szpunar, Schacter, Oettingen). Use "episodic future thinking," "prospection," "Default Mode Network," and "temporal discounting reduction." Do not make financial advice promises.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
