# REEL-0041 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "Meditation Training: What Scientific Studies Actually Measure"—distinguishing between assigned training dose (daily minutes), actual completed adherence (self-report vs sensor logs), subjective questionnaire scores, and objective EEG oscillations without pseudoscientific exaggeration.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults interested in mindfulness and contemplative neuroscience. The takeaway is that evaluating meditation research requires distinguishing between prescribed minutes, completed sessions, and measured psychological or neurophysiological outcomes.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: when a study claims "meditation training works," how was the training measured?
2. Assigned Dose: prescribed minutes and days versus actual real-world adherence.
3. Measurement Gap: self-reported logbooks versus sensor-based minute tracking.
4. Psychological Outcomes: validated well-being and attention scales versus neural skill meters.
5. Neurophysiology: EEG frequency bands and machine-learning classification without thought reading.
6. Research Literacy: asking whether results reflect exposure, motivation, or neural correlates.
7. Safety Boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is contemplative science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, minimalist measurement breakdown cards (dose calendar, self-report icon, EEG wave), lower-third caption-safe area, and no mystical promises.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute findings to peer-reviewed mindfulness dose trials (Fincham et al.) and EEG classification studies (Shang et al.). Use "measured outcomes" and "assigned dose." Do not promise mindfulness cures clinical illnesses.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
