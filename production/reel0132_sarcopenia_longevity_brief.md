# REEL-0132 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Sarcopenia & Muscle Mass Longevity: Type II Fast-Twitch Muscle Preservation, All-Cause Mortality Reduction, Glucose Disposal Sink, and Frailty Prevention"—how after age 30, humans naturally lose 3-8% of muscle mass per decade (sarcopenia), leading to insulin resistance, metabolic slowdown, and increased fall risks. Preserving muscle mass via progressive resistance training reduces all-cause mortality by over 40%. It attributes findings to Dr. Peter Attia and geriatric physiologists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone over 25 who prioritizes cardio over weights or neglects strength training. The takeaway is that muscle is the armor of longevity; having strong legs and grip strength is the single strongest predictor of independent, vigorous old age.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why muscle loss after age 30 is a bigger threat to long life than high cholesterol or wrinkles.
2. The Sarcopenia Timeline: the human body naturally losing up to 8% of skeletal muscle every 10 years without resistance stimuli.
3. Muscles as Glucose Sinks: 80% of dietary glucose being cleared directly into skeletal muscle; losing muscle leads directly to Type 2 diabetes.
4. Fast-Twitch Type II Fibers: aging selectively shrinking power fibers needed for balance, sudden recovery, and fall prevention.
5. 40% Mortality Reduction: large cohort studies showing high muscle mass and grip strength reducing all-cause mortality risk by 40-50%.
6. Practical Protocol: perform progressive resistance exercises (squats, deadlifts, push-ups) 2-3 times weekly and eat 1.6g protein per kg bodyweight.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute sarcopenia research to peer-reviewed literature (Attia, Cruz-Jentoft, Phillips, Volpi). Use "Sarcopenia Prevention," "Glucose Sink," "Type II Muscle Fibers," and "Longevity Biomarker."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
