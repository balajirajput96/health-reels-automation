# REEL-0166 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Dietary Fiber and Gut Microbiome: Soluble vs. Insoluble Fiber, Short-Chain Fatty Acids (SCFAs), and Metabolic Vitality"—how dietary fiber is not just 'roughage' for bowel movements, but the primary food source for trillion gut microbes that ferment it into butyrate and propionate, regulating blood sugar, reducing systemic inflammation, and strengthening the intestinal barrier. It attributes findings to the World Health Organization (WHO) and Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is health-conscious adults, fitness enthusiasts, and everyday diners looking to improve digestion and long-term vitality. The takeaway is that fiber feeds your microbial ecosystem; gradually adding a diverse mix of whole grains, legumes, fruits, and vegetables provides the substrate for life-supporting SCFAs.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why dietary fiber is not inert waste, but the single most critical fuel for trillions of bacteria in your gut.
2. The Microbiome Banquet: human digestive enzymes cannot break down prebiotic fiber, so it reaches the colon intact.
3. SCFA Superpower: microbial fermentation converting fiber into Short-Chain Fatty Acids, particularly Butyrate.
4. Intestinal Shield & Immunity: Butyrate fueling colonocytes, repairing gut lining, and moderating immune inflammation.
5. Metabolic Regulation: soluble fiber forming a viscous gel that slows glucose absorption and stabilizes insulin spikes.
6. The Diversity Rule: aim for 30+ different plant foods per week across beans, oats, seeds, and colourful vegetables.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute nutrition science to peer-reviewed public health literature (WHO, Harvard T.H. Chan, Sonnenburg, PMC). Use "Dietary Fiber," "Gut Microbiome," "Short-Chain Fatty Acids (SCFAs)," "Butyrate," and "Plant Diversity."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
