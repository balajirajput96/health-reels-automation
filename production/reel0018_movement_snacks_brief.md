# REEL-0018 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring "movement snacks"—short, frequent bouts of physical activity spread throughout the day—to break up prolonged sitting and support overall energy. It attributes findings to exercise physiology research from McMaster University and the National Institutes of Health (NIH).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is busy adults and desk workers seeking feasible ways to introduce physical activity into sedentary days. The takeaway is that brief movement intervals help interrupt sedentary time and support physical well-being.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: introducing the concept of movement snacks for desk workers.
2. Evidence: exercise physiology studies from McMaster and NIH.
3. Concept: 1-2 minute activity bouts (stairs, bodyweight squats, brisk walking).
4. Mechanism: breaking prolonged sitting and boosting circulation.
5. Feasibility: cumulative benefits of brief, frequent physical movements.
6. Safety boundary: reminding viewers to move within their current comfort level.
7. Medical disclosure: standard disclaimer that content is general education, not clinical prescription.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, active, modern workplace and home visuals showing low-friction movement (stair walking, desk stretches), lower-third caption-safe area, and no extreme fitness claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute exercise science to McMaster University and NIH. Use "may support" and "feasible option." Do not claim movement snacks cure cardiovascular or orthopedic conditions.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
