# REEL-0070 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "Self-Determination Theory: The Three Core Nutrients of Human Flourishing (Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness)"—how human motivation, vitality, and psychological well-being require fulfilling three innate psychological needs: Autonomy (feeling volition and ownership), Competence (feeling effective and capable), and Relatedness (feeling connected and cared for). It attributes findings to Edward L. Deci, Richard M. Ryan, and humanistic motivation science.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is leaders, employees, students, parents, and coaches. The takeaway is that external carrots and sticks (controlled motivation) deplete long-term drive; creating environments supporting autonomy, skill growth, and warm community unlocks enduring intrinsic motivation.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why forcing yourself to work purely for external praise or fear of punishment always ends in burnout.
2. The Theory: Edward Deci & Richard Ryan's Self-Determination Theory (SDT)—human thriving requires 3 psychological vitamins.
3. Nutrient 1 — Autonomy: the deep need to feel that our actions originate from our own authentic choice and values.
4. Nutrient 2 — Competence: the satisfying feeling of mastering skills, overcoming meaningful challenges, and growing.
5. Nutrient 3 — Relatedness: feeling valued, understood, and emotionally connected to a community.
6. Autonomous vs Controlled Motivation: autonomous self-authored drive produces deeper focus, creativity, and resilience.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is motivation psychology education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic Self-Determination Theory triad diagrams (Triad: Autonomy + Competence + Relatedness = Core Human Motivation), lower-third caption-safe area, and no business consulting promises.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute motivation science to peer-reviewed literature (Deci & Ryan, Vansteenkiste, Gagné). Use "Self-Determination Theory," "Autonomy," "Competence," "Relatedness," and "Autonomous Motivation." Do not offer clinical psychological therapy.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
