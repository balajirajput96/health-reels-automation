# REEL-0114 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Zone 2 Cardio & Mitochondrial Biogenesis: PGC-1alpha Activation, Lactate Clearance, Fat Oxidation, and Cellular Longevity"—how low-to-moderate intensity steady-state exercise (where you can talk in full sentences) stimulates the creation of new, highly efficient mitochondria (mitochondrial biogenesis), prevents metabolic dysfunction, and clears blood lactate. It attributes findings to Dr. Iñigo San Millán and Dr. Peter Attia.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone wanting to improve stamina, metabolic health, longevity, and fat burning without brutal exhaustion. The takeaway is that going "all-out" all the time harms mitochondrial efficiency; spending 150-180 minutes a week in Zone 2 is the gold standard for cellular powerhouse density.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why exercising so hard that you gasp for air is NOT the best way to build cellular stamina and burn body fat.
2. The Concept of Zone 2: aerobic intensity where fat is the primary fuel source and lactate production equals lactate clearance (1.5–2.0 mmol/L).
3. PGC-1alpha & Mitochondrial Biogenesis: Zone 2 triggering the master regulator PGC-1alpha to grow thousands of new mitochondria in muscle cells.
4. Lactate Shuttle & Cellular Cleaning: healthy mitochondria consuming lactate as fuel, preventing acid buildup and muscular fatigue.
5. The Conversational Pace Test: how to know you are in Zone 2—you can speak in full sentences, but you can't sing.
6. The Longevity Protocol: 3 to 4 sessions of 45 minutes per week of brisk walking, cycling, or light jogging.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute mitochondrial exercise physiology to peer-reviewed literature (San Millán, Attia, Brooks, Hawley). Use "Zone 2 Cardio," "Mitochondrial Biogenesis," "PGC-1alpha," and "Lactate Clearance."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
