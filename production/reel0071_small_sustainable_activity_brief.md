# REEL-0071 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel showing that starting physical activity does not require an all-or-nothing routine. It explains the WHO and CDC “start small and progress gradually” principle while avoiding fixed beginner prescriptions.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults who feel overwhelmed by formal exercise. The takeaway is to choose an accessible activity, place a realistic cue into the week, and progress gradually if appropriate. The reel is not individualized medical or exercise advice.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals animated with deterministic motion if official visual quotas permit. Native video generation is optional and may be used only if its official quota is available. If image generation remains exhausted, do not use unrelated visuals; record a blocked preproduction state.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: an adult looking at an intimidating workout plan, then choosing a simple comfortable movement option.
2. WHO principle: a calm sequence of small movement moments across an ordinary day, without generated numbers or guideline graphics.
3. CDC principle: a calendar-like routine with small separated activity moments, shown symbolically without text-bearing AI graphics.
4. No overnight target: a gradual path or staircase with modest steps, avoiding performance or body-transformation imagery.
5. Choose fit: inclusive adults choosing walking, gentle mobility, or another accessible activity in ordinary settings.
6. Sustainable cue: a person placing a simple reminder on a calendar and completing a comfortable movement break.
7. Safety: a person pausing and consulting a qualified professional when health context or symptoms make the choice uncertain.

## Visual constraints

Use a consistent realistic editorial documentary style with natural light, inclusive adult representation, varied abilities, neutral clothing, uncluttered backgrounds, lower-third caption-safe space, and no logos, fake guideline screenshots, diagnostic imagery, or text-bearing AI graphics. Do not portray exercise as a moral test or show dramatic body changes.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, use readable multiline blocks, remain bottom-centered, and avoid adding fixed times, distances, intensities, or targets not present in the approved script.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute “some is better than none,” gradual progression, smaller chunks, and starting slowly to WHO/CDC guidance. Do not claim that small steps guarantee long-term adherence, weight loss, disease prevention, or a particular health outcome. State that chronic conditions, pregnancy, injury, or concern about harm may warrant healthcare-professional guidance, and that concerning symptoms should not be ignored.

## Disclosure

If produced, state that visuals are AI-generated reference images animated with deterministic motion. Record any official quota limitation. The package remains unpublished until separate editorial review.
