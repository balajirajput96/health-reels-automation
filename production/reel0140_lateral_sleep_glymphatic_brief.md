# REEL-0140 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Glymphatic System Postural Dynamics: Lateral Sleep Posture, Aquaporin-4 (AQP4) Channels, Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF) Inflow, and 25% Higher Waste Clearance"—how sleeping on your side (lateral position) optimizes cerebrospinal fluid-interstitial fluid exchange along perivascular pathways, clearing 25% more toxic beta-amyloid and hyperphosphorylated tau proteins from the neocortex than supine (back) or prone (stomach) positions. It attributes findings to Dr. Maiken Nedergaard and Dr. Hedok Lee (University of Rochester / Stony Brook).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone interested in sleep quality, neuroprotection, and Alzheimer's prevention. The takeaway is that simply adopting a lateral (side) sleeping position creates the optimal gravitational and hydraulic pressure to flush metabolic toxins out of the brain during slow-wave sleep.
