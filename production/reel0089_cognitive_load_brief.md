# REEL-0089 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Cognitive Load Theory: Working Memory Capacity, Intrinsic vs Extraneous Load, and Avoiding Mental Overwhelm"—how the human working memory (located in the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex) can only hold about 4±1 chunks of information simultaneously, and how extraneous cognitive load (distractions, complex formatting) causes rapid mental fatigue and learning collapse. It attributes findings to John Sweller, George Miller, and cognitive psychologists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is students, programmers, managers, and anyone dealing with information overload. The takeaway is that reducing extraneous cognitive load (eliminating notifications, chunking complex concepts, single-tasking) preserves working memory capacity for deep problem-solving and permanent learning.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why trying to multitask or learn complex material in a noisy room makes your brain instantly freeze up.
2. Miller's Law & Cowan's Limit: working memory holding only 3 to 5 chunks of active information at any single moment.
3. The Three Types of Load: Intrinsic Load (inherent difficulty), Extraneous Load (wasteful distraction), and Germane Load (schema building).
4. The Extraneous Load Trap: notifications, messy layouts, and multitasking consuming working memory bandwidth without producing learning.
5. Cognitive Overload Signs: executive dysfunction, rapid mental exhaustion, and forgetting instructions immediately.
6. Cognitive Offloading Strategy: writing thoughts down (external memory), single-tasking, and breaking complex ideas into bite-sized chunks.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive load theory to peer-reviewed literature (Sweller, Miller, Cowan, Paas). Use "Cognitive Load Theory," "working memory," "intrinsic vs extraneous load," and "cognitive offloading." Do not diagnose learning disabilities or executive dysfunction.

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
