# REEL-0039 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring "Compassion Practice" (Loving-Kindness / Metta)—how intentional cultivation of prosocial benevolence engages neural caregiving circuits, modulates autonomic parasympathetic tone, and alters physiological markers of stress. It attributes findings to contemplative neuroscience and psychophysiology (Tania Singer et al., Barbara Fredrickson et al.).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults interested in contemplative science and emotional resilience. The takeaway is that compassion training strengthens neural caregiving networks and vagal regulation rather than being merely sentimental.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why wishing well for others physically calms our own cardiovascular system.
2. Evidence: contemplative neuroscience and psychophysiology research on compassion training.
3. Neural circuitry: activating the insula, anterior cingulate cortex, and caregiving pathways.
4. Autonomic modulation: enhancing heart rate variability (HRV) and reducing inflammatory stress markers.
5. Practice: brief, silent loving-kindness intentions towards self, friends, and neutral others.
6. Realist perspective: understanding that compassion is a trainable cognitive-affective skill developed gradually.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is contemplative science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, warm, minimalist physiological and neural network diagrams, lower-third caption-safe area, and no mystical or medical claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute contemplative science to peer-reviewed fMRI and autonomic compassion studies. Use "may engage" and "neural caregiving circuits." Do not claim compassion meditation cures clinical depression or cardiovascular disease.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
