# REEL-0129 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Spotlight Effect: Egocentric Bias, Social Anxiety Liberation, and Barry Manilow T-Shirt Experiment"—how human beings overestimate the extent to which other people notice their appearance, mistakes, or behavior by more than 50%, due to an evolutionary egocentric anchoring bias. It attributes findings to Dr. Thomas Gilovich and Dr. Kenneth Savitsky.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone held back by social anxiety, fear of judgment, or overthinking what others think of them. The takeaway is that nobody is watching you as closely as you think—everyone is stuck in their own internal spotlight; realize this and step into social freedom.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why tripping in public makes your face turn red because you think everyone saw you, when in reality almost nobody noticed.
2. The Barry Manilow T-Shirt Experiment: Thomas Gilovich having students wear an embarrassing t-shirt into a crowded room; students predicted 50% noticed, but only 23% actually did.
3. The Spotlight Effect: our brain mistakenly anchoring on our own intense self-awareness and projecting it onto everyone else.
4. Egocentric Brain Bias: the default assumption that we are the center of other people's universes.
5. The Truth About Others: other people are busy worrying about their own hair, pimples, mistakes, and insecurities.
6. The Freedom Mindset: whenever you feel self-conscious, remind yourself that everyone is looking at their own mirror, not at you.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute social psychology to peer-reviewed literature (Gilovich, Savitsky, Medvec, Epley). Use "The Spotlight Effect," "Egocentric Bias," "Barry Manilow Experiment," and "Social Anxiety Liberation."

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
