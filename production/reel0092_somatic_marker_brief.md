# REEL-0092 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Somatic Marker Hypothesis: Gut Feelings, Ventromedial Prefrontal Cortex (vmPFC), and Fast Emotion-Guided Decision Making"—how bodily signals (heart rate, gut sensations, skin conductance) serve as physiological markers that bias options before conscious reasoning takes place, enabling fast, effective real-world decisions. It attributes findings to Antonio Damasio and cognitive neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults who overthink decisions or wonder whether to trust their "gut feeling". The takeaway is that bodily gut feelings are not superstitious—they are high-speed summaries of past emotional learning stored in the vmPFC and insular cortex.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why your "gut instinct" often warns you about a bad decision seconds before your logical brain can explain why.
2. Damasio's Discovery: Antonio Damasio's landmark study showing patients with vmPFC damage making disastrous decisions despite intact IQ.
3. What is a Somatic Marker: physical body signals (heart rate change, stomach tightness) linked to past emotional outcomes.
4. Pre-Conscious Filtering: somatic markers rapidly pruning dangerous options before conscious calculation even starts.
5. Iowa Gambling Task Evidence: normal participants showing stress reactions in bodily signals before consciously knowing risky decks.
6. Combining Intuition & Logic: trusting initial bodily cues as data, then validating with prefrontal reasoning.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute somatic marker neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Damasio, Bechara, Tranel, Adolphs). Use "Somatic Marker Hypothesis," "vmPFC," "gut feelings," and "emotion-guided decision making." Do not claim intuition is infallible.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
