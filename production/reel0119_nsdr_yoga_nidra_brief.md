# REEL-0119 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR / Yoga Nidra): Restoring Striatal Dopamine by 65%, Theta-Delta Brainwaves, Accelerated Neuroplasticity, and Mental Recharging in 20 Minutes"—how guided body scanning and intentional breath control place the brain in a hypnagogic state between waking and sleeping, restoring neurotransmitter pools and consolidating learning without actual sleep. It attributes findings to Dr. Andrew Huberman, Dr. Kamakhya Kumar, and neuroimaging studies.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone suffering from afternoon fatigue, poor sleep the night before, or mental burnout. The takeaway is that a 20-minute NSDR session can recover up to 65% of striatal dopamine levels and accelerate the rate of motor and cognitive learning.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: how to completely recharge your brain's depleted dopamine and focus in just 20 minutes without drinking coffee or taking a nap.
2. What is Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR)?: an evidence-based protocol rooted in ancient Yoga Nidra involving conscious somatic relaxation.
3. Brainwave Shift to Theta & Delta: slowing down cortical electrical oscillations from high-frequency beta into restorative theta/delta states while retaining awareness.
4. 65% Striatal Dopamine Replenishment: PET scan studies showing deep conscious rest restoring dopamine levels in the ventral striatum.
5. Neuroplasticity Acceleration: NSDR immediately after a study/practice session accelerating the physical consolidation of new synaptic connections.
6. Daily Afternoon Protocol: lying down, putting in headphones, and doing a 10-20 minute guided NSDR body scan between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute NSDR neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Huberman, Kjaer, Kumar, Lou). Use "Non-Sleep Deep Rest," "Yoga Nidra," "Striatal Dopamine 65% Reset," and "Theta Wave Rest."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
