# REEL-0022 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining how cognitive science studies measure mindfulness and attention across three distinct layers: subjective questionnaires, behavioral tasks (e.g., Stroop), and electrophysiological markers (EEG/ERP). It attributes findings to cognitive psychology and neuroimaging research.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking a critical, evidence-informed understanding of mindfulness claims. The takeaway is that scientific studies measure specific performance and physiological correlates rather than a broad, universal attention boost.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: when someone claims meditation improves attention, ask what measure was used.
2. Layer 1: questionnaires measuring self-reported awareness and ratings.
3. Layer 2: behavioral tasks like Stroop measuring reaction times and accuracy.
4. Layer 3: EEG and ERP electrodes recording electrical activity correlates over time.
5. Study specifics: longitudinal randomized trials showing task-specific correlates.
6. Nuance: a change in one task metric does not prove universal cognitive enhancement.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is not clinical diagnosis.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, scientific, minimalist diagrammatic visuals (questionnaire icons, reaction time bars, ERP waveforms), lower-third caption-safe area, and no mystical or medical claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive science to peer-reviewed EEG and Stroop task studies. Use "task-specific correlate" and "measured outcome." Do not claim meditation cures ADHD, depression, or attention deficit disorders.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
