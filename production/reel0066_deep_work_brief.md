# REEL-0066 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "The Science of Deep Work and Sustained Attention: Cognitive Endurance vs Attention Residue"—how cognitive performance, vigilance, and error rates evolve over prolonged focus bouts, what laboratory studies actually measure (vigilance decrement, omission errors, reaction time, task-switching interruption costs), and the physiological limits of human ultradian 90-minute endurance. It attributes findings to Anders Ericsson, Cal Newport, and cognitive neuroscience.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is knowledge workers, programmers, researchers, and students trying to work 8-10 hours without breaks. The takeaway is that intense deep concentration is biologically capped at roughly 3-4 hours per day, best structured into 60-90 minute ultradian blocks with intentional cognitive downtime.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why forcing yourself to focus on a screen for 8 straight hours makes you slow, error-prone, and mentally drained.
2. What Studies Measure: laboratory vigilance tasks measuring accuracy decline, reaction time slowing, and omission errors.
3. Attention Residue: how switching to check one "quick email" leaves fragments of attention behind, degrading primary task depth.
4. Biological Ultradian Cycles: the brain's 90-minute natural wave of peak alertness followed by an autonomic rest trough.
5. The 4-Hour Daily Cap: Anders Ericsson's deliberate practice research showing elite violinists/coders cap maximum depth at 3-4 hours daily.
6. Optimal Deep Work Architecture: time-blocking single 60-90 minute distraction-free intervals followed by 15-minute complete detachment.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is cognitive ergonomics and productivity science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic cognitive endurance curves (90-min peak focus wave -> Attention residue fragmentation vs Clean block recovery), lower-third caption-safe area, and no business consulting promises.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive science to peer-reviewed attention literature (Ericsson, Newport, Leroy, Parasuraman). Use "deep work," "attention residue," "ultradian cycles," and "cognitive endurance." Do not make unconditional career guarantees.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
