# REEL-0090 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Confirmation Bias: Motivated Reasoning, Neural Identity Defense, and Why Believing You Are Right Feels So Good"—how the human brain selectively searches for, interprets, and remembers information that confirms existing beliefs while using motivated reasoning to dismiss opposing evidence. It attributes findings to Raymond Nickerson, Dan Kahan, and cognitive neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults navigating online debates, news feeds, and personal disagreements. The takeaway is that confirmation bias is an automatic cognitive shortcut designed to preserve identity and conserve mental energy; actively seeking disconfirming evidence ("red teaming" your own ideas) is the hallmark of genuine critical thinking.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why two intelligent people can watch the exact same debate or read the exact same report and come away 100% convinced of opposite conclusions.
2. The Mechanism: Confirmation Bias—the brain filtering incoming data through pre-existing mental models.
3. Motivated Reasoning: Dan Kahan's research showing how emotional attachment to an identity biases logical reasoning to protect group belonging.
4. Dopamine Spike for Being Right: the brain releasing dopamine when finding evidence that confirms existing beliefs.
5. The Backfire Effect: when presented with opposing facts, identity-defense circuits activate, making people double down on original beliefs.
6. The Remedy: Intellectual Humility and actively asking "What evidence would prove me wrong?"
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute confirmation bias research to peer-reviewed literature (Nickerson, Kahan, Lord, Lepper & Ross). Use "Confirmation Bias," "motivated reasoning," "identity defense," and "intellectual humility." Do not make political or controversial religious claims.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
