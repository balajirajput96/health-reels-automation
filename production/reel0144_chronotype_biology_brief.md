# REEL-0144 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Chronotype Genetics: PER3 Period Circadian Regulator Gene Polymorphism, Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN) Timing, and Why Night Owls Cannot Simply 'Wake Up at 5 AM'"—how human chronotypes (Morning Larks vs Night Owls) are genetically hardwired by variations in the PER3 clock gene and suprachiasmatic nucleus oscillation speeds, meaning waking up early is not a moral virtue but a biological phenotype. It attributes findings to Dr. Matthew Walker and circadian geneticists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone suffering from morning fatigue, night-owl guilt, or rigid 9-to-5 societal pressures. The takeaway is that your circadian peak is encoded in your DNA (PER3 gene); optimize your demanding cognitive work during your biological peak window rather than forcing artificial schedules.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why forcing a genetic night owl to wake up at 5 AM gives them the cognitive impairment of being legally drunk.
2. The PER3 Gene Clock: circadian timing governed by repeating genetic polymorphisms in the PER3 gene and SCN neurons.
3. Morning Larks (30% of population): melatonin shutting off at 5 AM, core body temperature rising early, peak focus at 9 AM.
4. Night Owls (30% of population): melatonin lingering until 9 AM, peak prefrontal cortex cognitive performance between 5 PM and 10 PM.
5. The Evolutionary Night Watch: primitive tribes needing members to sleep in staggered shifts to ensure 24-hour survival against predators.
6. The Chronotype Alignment Rule: discover your natural rhythm, schedule deep complex work during your biological peak, and protect 8 hours of sleep.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute circadian genetics to peer-reviewed literature (Walker, Archer, Roenneberg, Takahashi). Use "Chronotype Genetics," "PER3 Gene Polymorphism," "Suprachiasmatic Timing," and "Circadian Peak Windows."
