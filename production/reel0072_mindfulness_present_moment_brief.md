# REEL-0072 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining mindfulness as intentionally noticing present-moment experience without judgment. The reel focuses on a simple attention cue and clearly separates practice definition from uncertain health-outcome evidence.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults curious about mindfulness who may expect an instant fix. The takeaway is modest: notice one breath or sensory detail, notice when attention wanders, and gently return. The reel is not therapy, diagnosis, spiritual instruction, or a guarantee of calm.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals animated with deterministic motion if official visual quotas permit. Native video generation is optional and may be used only if its official quota is available. If image generation remains exhausted, do not use unrelated visuals; record a blocked preproduction state.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: an adult by a window notices a busy thought moment, then looks gently toward present surroundings; no distress imagery.
2. Definition: close, calm observation of hands, face, room light, and nearby environment without text or mystical symbols.
3. Attention anchor: a breath cue shown through subtle chest movement or a hand near the abdomen, plus a leaf or sound-source detail; no anatomical medical graphics.
4. Wandering is normal: soft visual transition from an abstract thought blur back to the room, without implying thought suppression.
5. Everyday practice: mindful walking or eating in a natural, ordinary environment; no religious or spiritual iconography.
6. Evidence boundary: neutral research notebook and calm everyday scene, signaling that evidence is not a guarantee; no fake study screenshots or numbers.
7. Safety: a person pausing and choosing professional support when practice feels uncomfortable or symptoms are significant; no crisis or diagnostic imagery.

## Visual constraints

Use a consistent realistic editorial documentary style with soft natural light, inclusive adult representation, neutral clothing, uncluttered environments, lower-third caption-safe space, and no logos, fake research graphics, religious claims, mystical energy effects, diagnostic imagery, or text-bearing AI graphics. Avoid portraying mindfulness as blanking the mind or achieving supernatural calm.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, use readable multiline blocks, remain bottom-centered, and avoid adding meditation durations, breathing counts, treatment claims, or spiritual assertions not present in the script.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute the definition and practical examples to NIH and Harvard Health. Describe health-outcome evidence as possible and person-dependent. Do not claim that mindfulness cures anxiety or depression, guarantees emotional regulation, replaces therapy, or works identically for everyone. Keep the “not treatment” boundary explicit.

## Disclosure

If produced, state that visuals are AI-generated reference images animated with deterministic motion. Record any official quota limitation. The package remains unpublished until separate editorial review.
