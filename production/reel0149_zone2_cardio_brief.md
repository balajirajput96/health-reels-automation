# REEL-0149 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Zone 2 Cardio Exercise: Mitochondrial Biogenesis, Fatty Acid Oxidation, Lactate Clearance, and Cellular Longevity"—how low-intensity aerobic training at a conversational pace (Zone 2, where blood lactate stays below 2.0 mmol/L) forces slow-twitch type I muscle fibers to multiply their mitochondrial density and burn pure fat for fuel, reversing insulin resistance and boosting longevity. It attributes findings to Dr. Iñigo San Millán and Dr. Peter Attia.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is runners, gym-goers, and anyone seeking cardiovascular longevity without excessive exhaustion. The takeaway is that going too hard all the time burns sugar and causes fatigue; doing 80% of your training at an easy conversational pace creates massive cellular energy powerplants.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why exercising at a slow pace where you can still talk in full sentences burns more fat than running until you gasp for breath.
2. The Zone 2 Physiology Discovery: Dr. Iñigo San Millán showing slow-intensity training targeting Type I muscle fibers.
3. The Mitochondrial Density Boom: stimulating mitochondrial biogenesis, the cellular powerplants of the body.
4. Pure Fatty Acid Beta-Oxidation: burning pure stored fat rather than relying on rapid glycogen/sugar depletion.
5. Lactate Clearance Capacity: training cells to clear metabolic waste before it turns into muscle burn and stiffness.
6. The Conversational Test Rule: jog, cycle, or brisk walk at a speed where you can talk comfortably without gasping, for 45 minutes, 3 times a week.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute exercise physiology to peer-reviewed literature (San Millán, Brooks, Attia, Seiler). Use "Zone 2 Cardio," "Mitochondrial Biogenesis," "Lactate Clearance," and "Fatty Acid Oxidation."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
