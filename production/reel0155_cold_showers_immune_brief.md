# REEL-0155 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Science of Cold Showers for Immune Resilience: Leukocyte Proliferation, Norepinephrine Shock, and The 29% Sickness Reduction"—how ending daily showers with 30 to 90 seconds of cold water triggers rapid sympathetic norepinephrine vasoconstriction, mobilizing white blood cells (leukocytes and monocytes) and reducing workplace sickness absences by 29%. It attributes findings to Dr. Geert A. Buijze (Academic Medical Center Amsterdam, PLOS ONE 2016).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone catching frequent seasonal colds, suffering from morning sluggishness, or seeking natural immune support. The takeaway is that a daily 30-to-60 second cold water blast primes your immune system and spikes mental alertness for hours.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why turning your shower tap to cold for the last 60 seconds cuts your chances of catching a cold by 30%.
2. The AMC Amsterdam Randomized Controlled Trial: Dr. Geert Buijze tracking 3,018 adults across 30, 60, and 90-second cold shower routines.
3. The 29% Sickness Absence Drop: subjects reporting a 29% decrease in sickness-related days off work compared to controls.
4. Leukocyte & Monocyte Mobilization: thermal shock stimulating bone marrow to release disease-fighting immune cells.
5. The Norepinephrine Alertness Surge: cold water on the neck and back spiking systemic norepinephrine by 200-300%, elevating mood and vigilance.
6. The 30-Second Finishing Habit: take your regular warm shower, then turn the dial to cold for the final 30 to 60 seconds.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute thermal immunology to peer-reviewed literature (Buijze, Siems, Brenke, Janský). Use "Cold Shower Immunology," "Leukocyte Mobilization," "PLOS ONE 2016 Study," and "Norepinephrine Shock."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
