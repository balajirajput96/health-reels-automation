# REEL-0013 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining that evening reflection and cognitive offloading (writing down thoughts before bed) can help calm pre-sleep thinking and support restorative sleep hygiene. It attributes findings to public health research published in PMC (PubMed Central) and Harvard Health.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking evidence-backed bedtime routines to ease evening mental load and support sleep quality. The takeaway is that writing down pending tasks or thoughts before bed creates psychological closure and signals rest.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: a calm evening scene of someone unwinding in soft dim light.
2. Evidence: PMC research reference on bedtime writing and cognitive offloading.
3. Mechanism: writing down thoughts to reduce pre-sleep cognitive load.
4. Support: Harvard Health insights on stress management and restorative sleep.
5. Practice: noting 2-3 pending items or reflections to create psychological closure.
6. Individual variation: recognizing that evening routines vary and can be adapted to personal pace.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is for awareness and not medical advice.

## Visual constraints

Use a realistic, warm-toned evening aesthetic with soft amber lighting, inclusive adult representation, lower-third caption-safe area, and no medical insomnia claims or drug promotion.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid clinical guarantees.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive offloading research to PMC (PMC5758411) and Harvard Health. Use "may help" and "supportive." Do not claim bedtime writing cures chronic insomnia or replaces medical evaluation.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
