# REEL-0162 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Creatine Monohydrate & Brain Bioenergetics: The Phosphocreatine Shuttle, ATP Mitochondrial Recycling, Working Memory, and Cognitive Resilience"—how creatine is not just for gym bodybuilding muscles, but serves as a crucial rapid-energy buffer in neurons, replenishing depleted ATP via creatine kinase during acute mental fatigue, sleep deprivation, and complex cognitive tasks. It attributes findings to Dr. Darren Candow and neuro-metabolic researchers.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is students, knowledge workers, vegetarians, and aging adults. The takeaway is that the brain consumes 20% of resting body energy; daily creatine monohydrate supplementation (3-5g) directly supports neuronal energy homeostasis and working memory under mental stress.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why creatine is known for building bodybuilder biceps, but top neuroscientists take it every day for brain power and memory.
2. Brain Energy Consumption Reality: the human brain weighing only 2% of body weight but consuming over 20% of daily glucose and ATP cellular energy.
3. The Phosphocreatine Neural Buffer: when neurons fire rapidly under mental workload, ATP loses a phosphate group; creatine kinase instantly donates its phosphate back to recreate ATP.
4. Sleep Deprivation & Cognitive Fatigue Shield: clinical trials showing creatine prevents mental fog and preserves working memory during sleep deficits.
5. Vegetarian & Vegan Cognitive Boost: plant-based diets having zero dietary creatine, showing significant memory improvements upon supplementation.
6. The Standard Brain Dose: 3 to 5 grams of pure creatine monohydrate daily with plenty of water.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute neuroenergetic physiology to peer-reviewed literature (Candow, Rae, Rawson, Smith). Use "Creatine Monohydrate," "Phosphocreatine Shuttle," "ATP Energy Recycling," and "Working Memory."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
