# REEL-0037 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring "Cue Competition" and associative learning in habit formation—how multiple environmental cues compete for associative strength, and how existing cues can block or overshadow new habit formation. It attributes findings to classical associative learning models (e.g., Rescorla-Wagner) and modern behavioral neuroscience.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults wondering why new habits fail when introduced in cluttered or distraction-heavy environments. The takeaway is that minimizing competing sensory triggers allows a new habit cue to establish clear associative control.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why starting a new reading habit on the sofa with a TV on usually fails.
2. Evidence: associative learning research on cue competition and overshadowing (Rescorla-Wagner).
3. Mechanism: the brain distributing limited predictive value among multiple concurrent cues.
4. Blocking effect: strong pre-existing habit cues (phone notifications) overpowering weak new cues.
5. Environmental design: establishing dedicated, clean contextual cues for new routines.
6. Contextual stability: keeping location and timing consistent to maximize associative strength.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is associative learning science.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, minimalist associative node diagrams comparing competing cues vs isolated single cues, lower-third caption-safe area, and no self-help hyperbole.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute behavioral neuroscience to peer-reviewed associative learning and conditioning studies. Use "associative strength" and "cue competition." Do not claim cue control solves all habit challenges.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
