# REEL-0141 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Neuroscience of Core Value Affirmation: Ventromedial Prefrontal Cortex (vmPFC) Activation, Cortisol Reduction, and Defensive Threat Buffering"—how spending 3 minutes reflecting on your core personal values (such as family, honesty, or growth) activates the ventral striatum and ventromedial prefrontal cortex, physically insulating the nervous system against acute social evaluation threats and cortisol spikes. It attributes findings to Dr. Claude Steele and Dr. David Creswell (Carnegie Mellon).

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is students facing exams, professionals preparing for high-stakes presentations, and individuals dealing with impostor syndrome. The takeaway is that affirming your core identity values before stressful events shields your brain from panic and cognitive degradation.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why writing down 3 things that truly matter to you before an interview or exam drops your stress hormones by 50%.
2. Claude Steele's Self-Affirmation Theory: the human brain defending its global self-integrity when facing acute threats.
3. David Creswell's fMRI Discovery: neural activation in the ventral striatum and ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC).
4. Cortisol Buffering Mechanism: vmPFC signaling the hypothalamus to blunt adrenal cortisol secretion during evaluation.
5. Ego Threat Neutralization: value affirmation shifting the brain from a defensive survival mindset to an open, problem-solving mindset.
6. The 3-Minute Pre-Performance Ritual: write down your top 2 values (e.g., family, learning) and 3 sentences on why they define you.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute psychological neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Steele, Creswell, Critcher, Cohen). Use "Core Value Affirmation," "vmPFC Activation," "Cortisol Threat Buffer," and "Self-Integrity."

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
