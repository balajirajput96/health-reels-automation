# REEL-0076 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "Cognitive Reappraisal vs Expressive Suppression: The Two Paths of Emotion Regulation in the Brain"—how James Gross's Process Model explains the difference between reframing an emotional event early in the generative cycle (Cognitive Reappraisal, activating vlPFC and dampening amygdala reactivity) versus bottling up emotional expression late in the cycle (Expressive Suppression, which leaves sympathetic arousal, blood pressure, and cortisol elevated). It attributes findings to James J. Gross, Kevin Ochsner, and affective neuroscience.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults managing interpersonal stress, anger, anxiety, workplace conflicts, and emotional overwhelm. The takeaway is that hiding feelings (suppression) drains executive energy and strains the cardiovascular system; reframing the narrative before reacting (reappraisal) calms the amygdala naturally.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why forcing a fake smile when you are boiling with anger inside actually makes your heart beat faster and raises your blood pressure.
2. The Two Paths: James Gross's Emotion Regulation model—Cognitive Reappraisal (early) vs Expressive Suppression (late).
3. The Cost of Suppression: hiding outward expression leaves the amygdala firing and elevates autonomic sympathetic stress.
4. The Power of Cognitive Reappraisal: reinterpreting the meaning of a negative trigger before the emotional reaction crystallizes.
5. Neural Mechanism: Ventrolateral Prefrontal Cortex (vlPFC) sending top-down inhibitory signals to quiet the Amygdala.
6. Practical Reappraisal Technique: asking "Is there another explanation?" or "What can this teach me?" before reacting.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is affective neuroscience and emotional psychology education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic Emotion Regulation comparison timelines (Trigger -> Reappraisal: vlPFC dampens Amygdala vs Suppression: Masked face with high Amygdala/Cortisol spikes), lower-third caption-safe area, and no psychiatric therapy claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute affective neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Gross, Ochsner, Richards, Buhle). Use "emotion regulation," "cognitive reappraisal," "expressive suppression," and "vlPFC-amygdala pathway." Do not diagnose bipolar disorder or major depressive episodes.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
