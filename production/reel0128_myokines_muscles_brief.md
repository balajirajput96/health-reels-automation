# REEL-0128 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Myokines: How Skeletal Muscles Act as an Endocrine Organ, Secreting Irisin and IL-6 to Protect the Brain and Burn Visceral Fat"—how muscle contractions during resistance and aerobic exercise produce and release signaling peptides (myokines) that cross the blood-brain barrier, stimulate hippocampal neurogenesis (BDNF), reduce systemic inflammation, and turn white fat into thermogenic beige fat. It attributes findings to Dr. Bente Klarlund Pedersen and Dr. Bruce Spiegelman.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone viewing exercise merely as burning calories. The takeaway is that muscles are your body's largest endocrine pharmacy; contracting them releases biochemical miracle molecules (myokines) that shield your brain from dementia and enhance mood.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why muscles are not just for lifting heavy objects—they are actually your body's most powerful hormone factory.
2. Bente Klarlund Pedersen's Discovery (2003): discovering that contracting skeletal muscles release specialized signaling proteins called Myokines.
3. Irisin & The Blood-Brain Barrier: the exercise hormone Irisin crossing into the brain and triggering massive BDNF release in the memory center (hippocampus).
4. Anti-Inflammatory IL-6: muscle-derived IL-6 acting as a potent anti-inflammatory agent, countering visceral fat inflammation.
5. White to Beige Fat Browning: myokines signaling stubborn white belly fat cells to convert into metabolically active fat-burning beige fat.
6. Daily Muscle Contraction Protocol: perform 20-30 minutes of resistance training or bodyweight squats to continuously pump myokines through your bloodstream.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute myokine endocrinology to peer-reviewed literature (Pedersen, Spiegelman, Febbraio, Wrann). Use "Myokines," "Endocrine Organ," "Irisin & BDNF," and "Muscle-Brain Pharmacy."

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
