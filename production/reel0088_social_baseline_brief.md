# REEL-0088 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Social Baseline Theory: Neural Co-Regulation, Hand-Holding Studies, and Metabolic Load Sharing"—how the human brain assumes proximity to trusted social partners as its baseline ecological state, and how physical or emotional connection down-regulates hypothalamic stress activity (lowering metabolic risk and baseline threat vigilance). It attributes findings to James Coan, Stephen Porges, and social neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults experiencing isolation, high baseline stress, or relationship difficulties. The takeaway is that human beings are neurologically hardwired to share emotional and metabolic burdens—being near trusted people literally reduces the energy required by the brain to handle stress.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why holding hands with someone you trust instantly reduces your reaction to physical pain or threat.
2. Social Baseline Principle: James Coan's theory that isolation, not social connection, is the metabolically expensive abnormal state for the brain.
3. The Hand-Holding fMRI Experiment: brain scans showing reduced hypothalamic and insular activation under threat when holding a partner's hand.
4. Metabolic Resource Sharing: the brain calculating that tasks are easier and threats are smaller when surrounded by a supportive group.
5. Neural Co-Regulation: autonomic nervous system alignment (vagal tone, heart rate variability) through eye contact and physical presence.
6. The Loneliness Penalty: chronic isolation requiring the brain to expend extra energy remaining on high threat alert.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute social baseline neuroscience to peer-reviewed literature (Coan, Porges, Beckes, House). Use "Social Baseline Theory," "co-regulation," "hypothalamus," and "metabolic load sharing." Do not diagnose depression or attachment disorders.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
