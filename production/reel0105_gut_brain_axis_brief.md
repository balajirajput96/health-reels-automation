# REEL-0105 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "The Gut-Brain Axis: The Microbiome-Vagus Superhighway, 90% Serotonin in the Gut, and Enteric Nervous System"—how 100 trillion gut microbes synthesize crucial neurotransmitters (serotonin, GABA, short-chain fatty acids) and communicate directly with the central nervous system via the vagus nerve to influence mood, anxiety, and mental clarity. It attributes findings to John Cryan, Emeran Mayer, and neurogastroenterology researchers.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults experiencing brain fog, mood fluctuations, or digestive issues linked to stress. The takeaway is that the gut is literally our "second brain"—what you feed your microbiome directly determines the neurotransmitters traveling up your vagus nerve to regulate emotional resilience.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why feeling "butterflies in your stomach" or feeling anxious is not all in your head, but originates directly in your digestive tract.
2. The Second Brain: the Enteric Nervous System (ENS) containing over 500 million neurons lining the gastrointestinal walls.
3. 90% of Serotonin: gut enterochromaffin cells and microbes producing over 90% of the body's total serotonin and large amounts of calming GABA.
4. The Vagus Nerve Superhighway: 80% of vagal nerve fibers carrying sensory information upstream from the gut to the brain stem (afferent signaling).
5. SCFAs & Blood-Brain Barrier: bacterial fermentation of dietary fiber producing butyrate and propionate that reinforce the blood-brain barrier.
6. Feeding the Second Brain: consuming prebiotic plant diversity and fermented foods to cultivate an anxiety-calming microbiome.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute gut-brain axis research to peer-reviewed literature (Cryan, Dinan, Mayer, Sonnenburg). Use "Gut-Brain Axis," "Enteric Nervous System (ENS)," "Vagus Nerve superhighway," and "Microbial Serotonin & GABA." Do not diagnose gastroenterological or psychiatric diseases.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
