# REEL-0098 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "BDNF and Physical Exercise: Miracle-Gro for the Brain, Hippocampal Neurogenesis, and Synaptic Plasticity"—how aerobic exercise triggers the release of Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) from astrocytes and neurons, promoting new neuron growth in the hippocampus, strengthening synaptic connections, and insulating against cognitive decline. It attributes findings to Carl Cotman, Henriette van Praag, and exercise neuroscientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is anyone looking to boost memory, focus, learning capacity, and mood. The takeaway is that physical exercise is not just for muscle and heart health—it is the single most potent non-pharmacological stimulus for growing new brain cells and protecting cognitive longevity.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why a 30-minute brisk walk or run clears brain fog and makes you absorb information twice as fast.
2. What is BDNF: Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor—a master protein often called "Miracle-Gro" for brain cells.
3. The Muscle-to-Brain Signal: working muscles releasing lactate and myokines (like irisin) that cross the blood-brain barrier and trigger BDNF synthesis.
4. Adult Neurogenesis: BDNF inducing new neuron proliferation in the dentate gyrus of the hippocampus.
5. Synaptic Strengthening & Memory: BDNF promoting dendritic spine growth and strengthening Long-Term Potentiation (LTP).
6. Mood & Resilience: BDNF countering stress-induced hippocampal atrophy and enhancing emotional stability.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute BDNF research to peer-reviewed literature (Cotman, van Praag, Mattson, Kramer). Use "BDNF," "hippocampal neurogenesis," "synaptic plasticity," and "myokines." Do not claim exercise replaces medical treatment for neurodegenerative diseases.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
