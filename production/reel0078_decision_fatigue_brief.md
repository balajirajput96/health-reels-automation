# REEL-0078 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "Decision Fatigue and Executive Function Depletion: Why Choosing Too Much Makes You Make Bad Choices"—how the prefrontal cortex's self-regulatory capacity diminishes after making a long sequence of conscious decisions (the ego-depletion and choice overload phenomenon), leading to impulsive purchases, default status-quo bias, and loss of self-control late in the day. It attributes findings to Roy Baumeister, Kathleen Vohs, and behavioral decision scientists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is consumers, managers, judges, students, and everyday adults suffering from evening decision exhaustion. The takeaway is that willpower and analytical choice-making are finite daily cognitive resources; routinizing trivial decisions (wardrobe, breakfast, daily routines) protects high-stakes decision quality.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why shopping in a supermarket for 30 minutes leaves you grabbing sugary junk food right next to the billing counter.
2. The Concept: Decision Fatigue—how every single choice (big or small) depletes prefrontal cognitive energy.
3. The Famous Judicial Study: Danziger et al.'s finding that parole approvals by judges dropped significantly before meal/rest breaks.
4. Two Coping Mechanisms: when depleted, the brain either acts impulsively (giving in to cravings) or defaults to doing nothing (Status Quo bias).
5. The Prefrontal Metabolic Drain: conscious deliberation consuming glucose and oxygen in the executive control network.
6. The Simplification Strategy: automate minor morning choices (Steve Jobs' uniform, meal prepping) to save energy for crucial priorities.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is cognitive ergonomics and behavioral decision science education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic Decision Fatigue depletion meters (Morning full battery -> 100 small decisions -> Red depleted battery -> Impulsive choice spike), lower-third caption-safe area, and no medical burnout claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute decision science to peer-reviewed literature (Baumeister, Vohs, Danziger, Iyengar). Use "decision fatigue," "prefrontal executive depletion," "status quo bias," and "choice architecture." Do not diagnose chronic fatigue syndrome.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
