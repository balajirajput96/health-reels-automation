# REEL-0067 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel exploring "Social Learning Theory and Vicarious Reinforcement: How Humans Learn by Observing Others"—how humans acquire complex behaviors, social norms, language patterns, and emotional reactions without direct trial-and-error conditioning, mediated by the 4-step modeling process (Attention, Retention, Reproduction, Motivation) and mirror neuron systems. It attributes findings to Albert Bandura (Bobo Doll Experiment), Giacomo Rizzolatti, and social cognitive neuroscience.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is parents, teachers, leaders, creators, and individuals wishing to master new skills. The takeaway is that humans are vicarious modeling creatures; surrounding yourself with competent peers and demonstrating desired behaviors directly shapes behavioral adoption far more than verbal lectures.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why a child learns to speak, use a smartphone, or express emotions simply by watching adults, without formal instruction.
2. The Theory: Albert Bandura's Social Learning Theory—learning through observation, imitation, and modeling.
3. The 4-Stage Modeling Engine: Attention (noticing) -> Retention (remembering) -> Reproduction (practicing) -> Motivation (desire to act).
4. Vicarious Reinforcement: learning from the rewards and punishments received by others rather than experiencing consequences directly.
5. Neural Basis — Mirror Neurons: premotor and parietal brain circuits firing both when performing an action and when watching someone else do it.
6. The Peer Environment Effect: how workplace and social circles unconsciously dictate personal standards and habits.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is social cognitive psychology education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic social modeling workflow diagrams (Observer -> Model Behavior -> Mirror Neuron Activation -> Vicarious Reproduction), lower-third caption-safe area, and no child therapy claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute social psychology to peer-reviewed literature (Bandura, Rizzolatti, Meltzoff). Use "Social Learning Theory," "vicarious reinforcement," "modeling," and "mirror neuron system." Do not diagnose developmental disorders.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
