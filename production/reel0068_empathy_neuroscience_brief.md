# REEL-0068 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "The Neural Basis of Empathy: The Shared Pain Matrix vs Cognitive Perspective Taking"—how the human brain processes another person's physical and emotional suffering through shared neural resonance (Anterior Insula and Anterior Midcingulate Cortex / ACC) and how affective empathy differs from cognitive empathy (Theory of Mind / TPJ / mPFC) and compassionate motivation. It attributes findings to Tania Singer, Jean Decety, and social affective neuroscience.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is caregivers, healthcare professionals, managers, partners, and general adults. The takeaway is that emotional empathy directly co-activates pain circuits (which can cause empathetic distress and burnout); training compassionate perspective-taking (caring without emotional contagion) creates sustainable helpfulness.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why seeing someone accidentally stub their toe or cry makes you physically wince and feel a knot in your stomach.
2. The Shared Pain Matrix: Tania Singer's fMRI discovery that seeing a partner in pain lights up the observer's Anterior Insula and ACC.
3. Sensory vs Affective Pain: the brain mirrors the emotional unpleasantness of pain, not the physical sensory location.
4. Two Types of Empathy: Affective Empathy (feeling with someone) vs Cognitive Empathy (understanding their point of view).
5. The Burnout Trap: chronic affective empathy without cognitive boundaries causing emotional exhaustion in doctors and caretakers.
6. Cultivating Compassion: shifting from raw distress mirroring to proactive warm compassion (medial prefrontal / ventral striatum circuitry).
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is affective neuroscience and social psychology education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic empathy neural circuits (Partner Pain -> Shared Insula & ACC activation -> Affective Distress vs Warm Compassion divergence), lower-third caption-safe area, and no psychiatric diagnosis.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute affective neuroscience to peer-reviewed empathy literature (Singer, Decety, Batson, Lamm). Use "neural basis of empathy," "shared pain matrix," "anterior insula," and "cognitive vs affective empathy." Do not diagnose autism or psychopathy.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
