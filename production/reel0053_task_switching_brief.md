# REEL-0053 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel examining "Task Switching Costs and the Cognitive Tax of Multitasking"—how the prefrontal cortex cannot truly process multiple complex cognitive tasks simultaneously, but rather rapidly shifts executive control, paying an unavoidable metabolic and temporal penalty (switch cost, reaction time slowdown, and error spikes). It attributes findings to Robert Rogers, Stephen Monsell, and David Meyer.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is students, knowledge workers, and multi-tab switchers. The takeaway is that multitasking is actually rapid task-switching that reduces work speed by 20-40% and increases errors; single-task batching is the scientifically proven path to deep productivity.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why checking a WhatsApp message while writing an email actually costs you 10 minutes of mental restart time.
2. The Multitasking Myth: the brain cannot execute two conscious, goal-directed tasks in parallel.
3. Switch Cost Science: reaction time slows down and error rates double during rapid task alterations.
4. Prefrontal Reconfiguration: the metabolic effort required by executive networks to unload rule-set A and load rule-set B.
5. Attention Residue: residual thoughts from the prior task lingering and polluting current focus (Sophie Leroy).
6. Time-Batching Solution: grouping similar tasks into unbroken 30-to-60 minute uninterrupted focus blocks.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is cognitive ergonomics and psychology education.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic task-switching timeline cards (Task A -> Switch penalty gap / Red Cogwheel friction -> Task B -> Attention residue drag), lower-third caption-safe area, and no medical claims.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute cognitive science to peer-reviewed task-switching literature (Rogers & Monsell, Rubinstein, Meyer & Evans, Leroy). Use "task switching cost," "attention residue," and "prefrontal reconfiguration." Do not promise superhuman productivity.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
