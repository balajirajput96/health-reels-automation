# REEL-0038 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining "Environmental Friction" (The Effort Tax)—how small physical or logistical barriers (friction) disproportionately decrease unwanted behaviors, while reducing friction exponentially increases adherence to desirable habits. It attributes findings to behavioral ergonomics and environmental psychology.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults struggling with digital distractions, snacking, or skipped workouts. The takeaway is that adjusting physical distance and steps of effort shapes habits far more effectively than relying on willpower alone.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why putting your phone in another room cuts screen time by half.
2. Evidence: behavioral economics and environmental psychology research on physical friction.
3. Mechanism: the brain's baseline energy-conservation bias prioritizing the lowest-friction action.
4. Positive friction: adding 2-3 physical steps to interrupt impulsive, undesirable habits.
5. Negative friction: pre-positioning workout clothes or water bottles to make positive choices effortless.
6. Contextual balance: finding practical modifications that fit realistic daily living.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is behavioral design science.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, graphic friction meters and physical distance diagrammatic cards, lower-third caption-safe area, and no magic guarantees.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute behavioral science to peer-reviewed environmental friction and habit studies (Wendy Wood et al.). Use "may reduce friction" and "behavioral ergonomics." Do not present friction design as an instant cure-all.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
