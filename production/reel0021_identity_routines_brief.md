# REEL-0021 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining how aligning daily habits with core personal values and self-identity fosters sustainable behavior change over time. It attributes findings to psychological research published in PMC and behavioral science journals.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults looking to build lasting daily habits without relying solely on transient willpower. The takeaway is that connecting habits to personal identity provides intrinsic motivation and stability.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why willpower alone often fails to maintain new routines.
2. Evidence: psychological research linking habits to self-identity and values.
3. Mechanism: seeing each small action as a reflection of desired identity.
4. Support: focusing on identity-aligned cues rather than just distant outcome goals.
5. Environmental cues: small daily cues fostering automatic positive habits.
6. Individual variation: acknowledging that habit formation timelines vary by individual.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is not psychological or clinical therapy.

## Visual constraints

Use clean, minimalist workspace and lifestyle imagery (open notebooks, plants, calm ambient light), lower-third caption-safe area, and no self-help hyperbole.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid rules.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute behavioral science to PMC studies on identity-based habits. Use "may support" and "gradual process." Do not claim identity alignment cures mental health disorders.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
