# REEL-0164 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel on "Nasal Nitric Oxide: Paranasal Sinuses, Pulmonary Vasodilation, 15% Higher Blood Oxygenation, and The Humming Frequency Boost"—how breathing through your nose (and gentle humming) releases massive concentrations of nitric oxide (NO) gas produced continuously in the paranasal sinuses, dilating bronchial airways and lung blood capillaries to increase arterial oxygen uptake by 10 to 18% compared to mouth breathing. It attributes findings to Nobel laureate Dr. Louis Ignarro and respiratory physiologists.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is mouth breathers, athletes, snorers, and individuals seeking better endurance and lung function. The takeaway is that mouth breathing is for emergency eating, not breathing; nasal breathing floods your lungs with natural nitric oxide, sterilizing air and boosting blood oxygen.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: why breathing through your mouth during the day or at night deprives your lungs of a magical gas that boosts blood oxygen by 15%.
2. The Paranasal Sinus Nitric Oxide Factory: nasal cavities synthesizing continuous high concentrations of endogenous NO gas.
3. Pulmonary Vasodilation Mechanism: inhaled nasal nitric oxide traveling straight into lung alveoli, relaxing smooth muscle and dilating capillary blood vessels.
4. Ventilation-Perfusion Matching: lower lobes receiving optimized blood flow and oxygen extraction, increasing SpO2 arterial saturation without extra effort.
5. The 15x Humming Surge (Bhramari Pranayama): Swedish Karolinska Institute research proving humming sound vibrations increase sinus nitric oxide release by 15-fold.
6. The Nasal Breathing Habit: keep your mouth closed at rest and try 2 minutes of gentle humming exhalations to flood your lungs with oxygen.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure.
