# REEL-0014 Production Brief

## Concept

A Hindi general-education reel explaining that regular daytime outdoor light exposure acts as a natural environmental cue supporting circadian rhythms, daytime alertness, and sleep quality. It attributes findings to public health insights from the National Institutes of Health (NIH/NIGMS) and peer-reviewed research in PMC.

## Audience and takeaway

The audience is adults seeking evidence-backed ways to support their natural sleep-wake cycle through simple lifestyle habits. The takeaway is that spending time outside in natural daylight during the day helps align body clocks and support overall well-being.

## Format

Portrait 9:16 video, approximately one minute, with Hindi off-screen narration, synchronized Hindi captions, and topic-specific reference visuals.

## Seven-span visual plan

1. Hook: a bright daylight outdoor scene showing someone stepping outside.
2. Evidence: NIH/NIGMS circadian rhythm research reference.
3. Mechanism: natural light helping align internal body clocks.
4. Support: PMC research insights on daytime outdoor exposure and sleep quality.
5. Daily routine: incorporating brief outdoor walks or breaks during daylight hours.
6. Individual variation: recognizing that daylight availability and individual schedules vary.
7. Safety boundary: standard educational disclosure stating content is for awareness and not medical advice.

## Visual constraints

Use a realistic, sunlit editorial documentary style with soft natural lighting, inclusive adult representation, lower-third caption-safe area, and no medical claims or clinical guarantees.

## Caption policy

Captions must reproduce the approved Hindi narration, remain bottom-centered, use readable multiline blocks, and avoid rigid prescriptions.

## Evidence and safety gates

Attribute circadian rhythm research to NIH/NIGMS and peer-reviewed studies in PMC. Use "may help" and "supportive." Do not claim daylight exposure cures circadian rhythm disorders or replaces medical consultation.

## Disclosure

State that content is for general educational awareness. The package remains subject to standard repository verification.
